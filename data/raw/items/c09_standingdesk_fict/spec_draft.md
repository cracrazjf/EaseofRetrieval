The Ostreno D2 is a height-adjustable desk frame for home and office use. The frame travels from 24.4 to 49.6 inches on two-stage lifting columns driven by a single motor at about one inch per second, a range narrower at both ends than three-stage frames reach. It carries 265 pounds, which rules out the heaviest desk setups. Construction is powder-coated steel, and the assembled frame weighs about 62 pounds. An LED keypad stores two memory presets and includes a child lock. The frame has no obstacle sensing, so travel continues until the keypad is released or a memory height is reached, even if something is in the way. Sound during travel stays under about 55 decibels, audible in a shared room. Cable management amounts to a set of adhesive clips rather than a tray. The frame carries a ten year warranty on the frame and five on the motor, and takes whatever desktop the buyer supplies. Included accessories: the keypad, mounting hardware, and the cable clips. Maintenance involves checking the column bolts, keeping the travel path clear, and wiping the frame with a dry cloth. The desktop is bought separately and bolts to the frame through pre-drilled mounting points along both crossbars.
