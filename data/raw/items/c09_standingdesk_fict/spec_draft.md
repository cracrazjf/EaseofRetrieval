The Ostreno D2 is a height-adjustable desk frame for home and office use. The frame travels from 24.4 to 49.6 inches on two-stage lifting columns driven by a single motor at about one inch per second. It is rated to carry 265 pounds of load. Construction is powder-coated steel, and the assembled frame weighs about 62 pounds. An LED keypad stores two memory heights and includes a child lock. The frame has no obstacle sensing, so travel continues until the keypad is released or a memory height is reached. Sound during travel stays under about 55 decibels. Included accessories: the keypad, mounting hardware, and a set of adhesive cable clips. Maintenance involves periodically checking the column bolts, keeping the travel path clear of obstructions, and wiping the frame and columns with a dry cloth after any spill. The frame ships flat and is assembled by the user.
