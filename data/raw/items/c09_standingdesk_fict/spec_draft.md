The Ostreno D2 is a height-adjustable desk frame for home and office use. The frame travels from 24.4 to 49.6 inches on two-stage lifting columns, a range cut short at both ends against three-stage frames. A single motor drives both columns in step at about one inch per second. The frame carries 265 pounds, which rules out the heaviest desk setups. Construction is powder-coated steel, and the assembled frame weighs about 62 pounds. The frame widens to fit desktops from 43 to 63 inches, and levelling feet on both bases take up the slack on an uneven floor. An LED keypad shows the current height as a number, stores two memory presets, and includes a child lock. The frame has no obstacle sensing, so travel continues until the keypad is released or a memory height is reached, even if something is in the way. Sound during travel stays under about 55 decibels, audible in a shared room. Cable management amounts to a set of adhesive clips instead of a tray. The frame carries a ten year warranty on the frame itself, while the motor is covered for five years. The keypad mounts under the front edge of the desktop, and travel across the full range takes about twenty-five seconds at the rated speed. The columns are powder-coated in black or white. Included accessories: the keypad, mounting hardware, an assembly tool, and the cable clips. The clips stick to the underside of the desktop, since no cable channel is built into the frame. Maintenance involves checking the column bolts, keeping the travel path clear, and wiping the frame with a dry cloth. The desktop is bought separately, and the frame takes whatever desktop the buyer supplies, bolting through pre-drilled mounting points along both crossbars.
