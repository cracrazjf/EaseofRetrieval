Stillbrook is a focus timer for desktop work. During a session an illustrated scene gradually fills in with detail, and the detail already earned is kept when the session is paused or abandoned early. While a session runs, the application blocks a user-selected list of desktop applications and websites; there is no mobile client, so phone use during a session is unrestricted, and no client exists for tablets either. An ambient sound library plays alongside the timer. Session history is recorded and summarised by day and week. The free tier covers the timer, the blocking list, three ambient tracks, and seven days of history. The paid tier adds the full sound library, unlimited history, and additional scene variants. There are no shared or group sessions of any kind. Clients run on macOS and Windows, with a companion web dashboard for reviewing statistics. Session data exports as CSV from the dashboard, and the blocking list can be edited between sessions but not while one is running. Statistics gather in the dashboard by day and by week, showing total focused time, session counts, and which applications were blocked most often during those sessions.
