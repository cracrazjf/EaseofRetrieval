Voskell is a budgeting application built around the envelope method, in which income is divided among named envelopes and any unspent amount rolls into the following period, which requires the envelopes to be reconciled each month. The service runs on the web and on iOS; there is no Android client yet. Transactions arrive by manual entry or by importing a CSV file exported from a bank, a recurring monthly step, since the app does not link to bank accounts directly. Entries can be recorded offline and sync when the device reconnects. Savings goals accept target dates, and monthly reports summarise category totals alongside a rollover history view; those reports cover categories without net worth tracking. A free tier covers ten envelopes and one account. The paid tier removes those limits and adds reports and sharing with one additional member and no more. Envelope and transaction data export as CSV files. Maintenance of a plan involves importing bank statements, reconciling the envelopes against them, and adjusting target dates as goals change each month. Every envelope carries a name, a target amount, and a running balance, and the rollover view shows how those balances moved across earlier periods.
