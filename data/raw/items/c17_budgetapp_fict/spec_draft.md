Voskell is a budgeting application built around the envelope method: income is divided among named envelopes, and any unspent amount rolls into the following period rather than resetting. Clients run on the web and on iOS; there is no Android client, and on a desktop the application is reached through the browser, with no installed desktop client. The application does not link to bank accounts. Transactions are typed in by hand or imported from a CSV file exported from a bank, and that import accepts one fixed column layout, so statements in other layouts are reshaped before they load. Entries can be recorded offline and sync when the device reconnects. Savings goals accept target dates for planned purchases. Recurring transactions are scheduled once and posted automatically each period. Envelope templates copy a plan from one month to the next. Receipt photos attach to individual transactions. Search runs across transactions by payee, amount, and date. Envelopes can be denominated in more than one currency within the same plan. A rollover history view shows how envelope balances moved across earlier periods. Monthly reports summarise category totals; those reports cover categories only, with no net worth tracking, and they open in the web app only, not on iOS. A free tier covers ten envelopes and one account without payment, and stops there. The paid tier lifts the envelope limit and adds sharing with one additional member and no more. Envelope and transaction data export as CSV files. Maintenance of a plan involves importing statements, reconciling the envelopes against them, and adjusting target dates as goals change. Each import is previewed before the transactions commit to the envelopes. Every envelope carries a name, a target amount, and a running balance that the rollover view tracks across periods.
