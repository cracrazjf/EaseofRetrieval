Trelbin is a note-taking application built around outline documents whose sections collapse and expand and can be linked to one another. Documents are stored locally on the device by default, and an optional encrypted sync service copies them between installations. Because storage is local, full editing and document creation work offline without any connection. A free tier covers unlimited local documents, syncing across two devices, and attachments up to 20 megabytes. The paid tier removes the device limit and adds version history and full-text search across attachment contents. Search across the local library is available on both tiers. Documents export as Markdown, plain text, or PDF files. Clients exist for macOS and Windows on the desktop and for iOS and Android on mobile; there is no browser client, and individual documents cannot be shared with people outside the account.
