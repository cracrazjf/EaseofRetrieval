The iRobot Roomba i7 is a robot vacuum for household floors. The unit is 13.34 inches wide and 3.63 inches high and weighs 7.44 pounds. A lithium ion battery gives 75 minutes of runtime and recharges in 1.5 hours at the dock, which the robot returns to on its own. The onboard bin holds 0.4 litres, and the Clean Base empties it automatically, holding up to 60 days of debris. Camera-based vSLAM navigation drives the robot in rows and Imprint Smart Mapping stores up to ten floor plans, so single rooms can be cleaned on request; the bumper still contacts furniture legs rather than easing around them. Dual rubber brushes resist hair tangling, though suction leaves ground-in dirt in carpet pile after a pass. It climbs thresholds up to 20 millimetres. The iRobot Home app schedules cleans and Alexa and Google Assistant start it by voice. Independent measurements place running noise at 65 to 68 decibels, with the self-empty cycle louder still. Disposal bags are a recurring purchase, and the robot is priced above basic models. Maintenance involves emptying the base and clearing the brushes. Included accessories: the Clean Base, a starter set of disposal bags, an extra filter, and a side brush.
