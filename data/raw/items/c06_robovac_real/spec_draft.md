The iRobot Roomba i7 is a robot vacuum for automated home floor cleaning. It measures 13.34 inches in diameter and 3.63 inches in height, and weighs 7.44 pounds. A lithium-ion battery runs up to 75 minutes per charge and recharges in about 1.5 hours; the robot returns to its dock on its own and resumes unfinished cleaning jobs. Camera-based vSLAM navigation moves the robot in orderly rows, and Imprint Smart Mapping stores up to 10 floor plans for cleaning specific rooms. Two rubber brush rolls pick up debris, the dustbin holds 0.4 liters, and the robot crosses thresholds up to 20 millimeters. Scheduling and control run through the iRobot Home app. A Clean Base self-emptying dock, bundled in the i7+ configuration, holds up to 60 days of debris in disposal bags. Maintenance involves emptying the dustbin and replacing Clean Base disposal bags when full.
