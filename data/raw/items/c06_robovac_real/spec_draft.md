The iRobot Roomba i7 is a robot vacuum for household floors. The unit measures 13.34 inches across and 3.63 inches high and weighs 7.44 pounds, and it passes under furniture that clears that height. A lithium ion battery gives 75 minutes of runtime per charge. When the charge runs down the robot returns to the dock on its own, recharges in 1.5 hours, and resumes the job where it stopped. The onboard dustbin holds 0.4 litres and fills faster on a dirty floor. In the i7+ configuration the Clean Base dock empties that bin into a disposal bag that holds up to 60 days of debris; the bags are proprietary and are bought again as they fill. Camera-based vSLAM navigation drives the robot in orderly rows, and Imprint Smart Mapping stores up to ten floor plans, so a single room can be cleaned on request. Dual rubber brush rolls resist hair tangling. The robot climbs thresholds up to 20 millimetres and stops at anything higher. The iRobot Home app handles scheduling and room selection, and Alexa and Google Assistant start a run by voice. Running noise reaches 65 to 68 decibels, and the Clean Base emptying cycle is louder than the robot itself. Suction leaves ground-in dirt in carpet pile after a pass, and the bumper knocks into furniture legs instead of easing around them. Hard floors and carpet are covered in one run, with no change of setting between them. The robot joins a 2.4 gigahertz Wi-Fi network, and the map of each run appears in the app once the job ends. Included accessories: the Clean Base, a starter set of disposal bags, a spare filter, and a side brush. Maintenance involves emptying the base bag and clearing the brush rolls. The robot does not mop or wet-clean, and it carries no forward obstacle-recognition camera, so cables and small objects on the floor are not identified before contact.
