The Garmin Forerunner 55 is a GPS running watch, now listed as discontinued by the maker. The case measures 42 by 42 by 11.6 millimetres, weighs 37 grams, and fits wrists between 126 and 203 millimetres. The 1.04-inch transflective colour display shows 208 by 208 pixels and stays readable in sunlight behind a chemically strengthened glass lens; it is small and low resolution against newer watches. The silicone band, the only material offered, uses a 20-millimetre quick release fitting. Battery life is up to 14 days in smartwatch mode or 20 hours with GPS running. Positioning uses GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo, with traces accurate across independent test routes, alongside a wrist heart rate sensor reading close to chest strap figures, an accelerometer, and 32 megabytes of history memory. There is no barometric altimeter, so no elevation data is recorded, no onboard music storage, no contactless payment, and no sleep summary on the watch itself. It pairs with iPhone and Android over Bluetooth and ANT+, carries a 5 ATM rating, and operates from -20 to 60 degrees Celsius. PacePro pacing and daily suggested workouts guide new runners. Included accessories: a charging clip cable and a quick start manual. The case is offered in one 42-millimetre size only.
