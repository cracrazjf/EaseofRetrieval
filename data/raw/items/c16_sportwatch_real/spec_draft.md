The Garmin Forerunner 55 is a GPS running watch. The case measures 42 x 42 x 11.6 mm, fits wrists from 126-203 mm, and weighs 37 g. The 1.04-inch color memory-in-pixel display has a resolution of 208 x 208 pixels and sits under a chemically strengthened glass lens; the 20 mm silicone band uses quick-release pins. Battery life is up to 14 days in smartwatch mode and up to 20 hours in GPS mode; the rechargeable lithium battery charges over a proprietary cable. The water rating is 5 ATM, and pool swimming is a supported activity. Positioning uses GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo satellites. Sensors include the Elevate optical heart-rate sensor and an accelerometer. Training functions include PacePro pacing strategies and daily suggested workouts. Data syncs over Bluetooth and ANT+, and internal memory is 32 MB. The watch was released in 2021 and is listed as discontinued on the official US product page.
