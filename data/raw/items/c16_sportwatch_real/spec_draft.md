The Garmin Forerunner 55 is a GPS running watch, now listed as discontinued. The case measures 42 by 42 by 11.6 millimetres, weighs 37 grams, and fits wrists between 126 and 203 millimetres; it is offered in one 42-millimetre size. The 1.04-inch transflective colour display shows 208 by 208 pixels behind a chemically strengthened glass lens and stays readable in direct sunlight, though beside newer watches the panel is small and low in resolution. The silicone band, the only material offered, uses a 20-millimetre quick release fitting that swaps without tools. Battery life reaches 14 days in smartwatch mode and 20 hours in GPS mode, a ceiling that limits very long events. Charging uses a proprietary clip cable rather than a standard connector. Positioning draws on GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo satellites, and the recorded traces stay accurate across varied routes; the wrist heart rate sensor reads close to chest strap figures. An accelerometer and 32 megabytes of history memory complete the sensor set. There is no barometric altimeter, so no elevation data is recorded. There is no onboard music storage. There is no contactless payment. No sleep summary appears on the watch itself; sleep figures are read in the phone application instead. The watch pairs with an iPhone or an Android phone over Bluetooth and ANT+, carries a 5 ATM water rating for swimming, and operates from -20 to 60 degrees Celsius. PacePro pacing, daily suggested workouts for new runners, and a track mode are included, and the watch records running, cycling, pool swimming, and indoor sessions. Included accessories: the proprietary charging clip cable and a quick start manual. Maintenance involves rinsing the case after swimming and keeping the charging contacts on the underside clear of salt and grit.
