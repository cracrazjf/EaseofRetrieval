The Garmin Forerunner 55 is a GPS running watch. The case measures 42 by 42 by 11.6 millimetres, weighs 37 grams, and fits wrists between 126 and 203 millimetres. The 1.04-inch transflective colour display shows 208 by 208 pixels and stays readable in sunlight behind a chemically strengthened glass lens. The silicone band uses a 20-millimetre quick release fitting. Battery life is rated at up to 14 days in smartwatch mode or 20 hours with GPS running. Positioning uses GPS, GLONASS, and Galileo, alongside a wrist heart rate sensor and an accelerometer, with 32 megabytes of history memory. The watch pairs with iPhone and Android handsets over Bluetooth and ANT+. It carries a 5 ATM water rating for swimming and operates from -20 to 60 degrees Celsius. Training features include PacePro pacing, daily suggested workouts, and a track mode. Included accessories: a charging clip cable and a quick start manual.
