The Ardento PB10 is a portable battery for phones and small devices. Capacity is 10,000 milliamp-hours, or 37 watt-hours. A USB-C port handles both input and output, and a second USB-C cable is built into the housing, stowing in a moulded channel along one edge. Two devices can therefore charge at once, one on the port and one on the attached cable. The port delivers 5 volts at 3 amps, up to 20 watts, and accepts the same rate as input; a 20-watt charger refills the pack in about four hours. A digital display on the front face shows the remaining charge as a percentage. The pack measures 104 by 62 by 25 millimetres and weighs 210 grams. It carries a two-year limited warranty. Maintenance involves keeping the port and the cable channel free of lint, and recharging the pack when it is stored unused for long periods.
