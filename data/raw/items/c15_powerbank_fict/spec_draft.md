The Ardento PB10 is a portable battery for phones and small devices. Capacity is 10,000 milliamp-hours, or 37 watt-hours, under the 100 watt-hour limit airlines apply to cabin baggage. A USB-C port handles both input and output, and a second USB-C cable is built into the housing and stows in a moulded channel along one edge that keeps it flat in a bag. Because that cable is attached, it cannot be replaced if it fails, and at 15 centimetres it reaches only far enough to sit a phone beside the pack. Two devices charge at once, one on the port and one on the attached cable, and there is no USB-A port for older cables. The attached cable ends in a USB-C plug, so a device with an older port needs an adapter that is not supplied. The port delivers 5 volts at 3 amps, up to 20 watts, and accepts the same rate as input; a 20-watt charger brings the pack back to full in about four hours, and the pack passes power through to a phone while it is itself charging. There is no low-current mode, so earbuds and other small loads fall below the current the pack needs to stay awake. A digital display on the front face shows the remaining charge as a percentage and drains the pack while it is lit. Over-current, over-voltage and temperature protection sit in the cell circuit. The pack measures 104 by 62 by 25 millimetres and weighs 210 grams, which weighs down a jacket pocket. It carries a two-year limited warranty. Included accessories: none beyond the attached cable; no wall charger is supplied. Maintenance involves keeping the port and the cable channel free of lint and recharging the pack when it is stored unused.
