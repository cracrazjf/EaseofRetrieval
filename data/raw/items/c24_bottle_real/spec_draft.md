The Thermos Stainless King is a vacuum-insulated beverage bottle. The 16-ounce size holds 475 millilitres, measures 9.7 by 3.0 by 3.0 inches, and weighs 0.9 pounds empty. The body is 18/8 stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation and BPA-free components. Hot retention is 18 hours and cold retention 24 hours. The bottle led the field for retention, holding 106 degrees Fahrenheit at 24 hours; contents are warm instead of hot by the end of a full day. A twist-and-pour stopper lets liquid out without removing the entire lid, and the lid itself is a built-in serving cup; the cup lid and the pour-through stopper are convenient in the field. There is no drinking spout and no straw lid, so the contents are poured into the cup. The cap seals leak-proof when closed. The exterior stays sweat-proof, so it leaves no ring on a table, and the bottle fits most vehicle cup holders, though the wide ridged body is harder to fit into a small bag. The plastic handle and stopper are less robust than the steel body. At 9.7 inches the bottle stands tall in a pack, and the weight is high for the capacity it carries. It is priced below comparable vacuum bottles. The stopper and the cup are moulded plastic and thread onto the steel body, and the same stopper and cup design carries across the sizes in the Stainless King line, of which the 16-ounce bottle is the smallest. The bottle is offered in several exterior colours. Included accessories: the serving cup lid, which is the only part supplied beyond the bottle and its stopper. Maintenance involves washing the body on the top rack of a dishwasher, or by hand, and drying the stopper threads and the cup separately before the parts are reassembled for storage.
