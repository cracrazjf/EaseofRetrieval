The Thermos Stainless King is a vacuum-insulated beverage bottle. The 16-ounce size holds 475 milliliters, measures 9.7 by 3.0 by 3.0 inches, and weighs 0.9 pounds. The body is 18/8 stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation and BPA-free components. Thermos states hot retention of 18 hours and cold retention of 24 hours. A twist-and-pour stopper lets liquid out without removing the entire lid, and the cap seals leak-proof when closed. The exterior stays sweat-proof and fits most vehicle cup holders. The bottle is offered in several exterior colors, and the same stopper and cup design carries across the sizes in the line. Included accessories: a serving cup built into the lid. Maintenance involves washing the body on the top rack of a dishwasher, or by hand with warm water, and drying the stopper threads and the serving cup separately before the parts are reassembled for storage.
