The Thermos Stainless King is a vacuum-insulated beverage bottle. The 16-ounce size holds 475 millilitres, measures 9.7 by 3.0 by 3.0 inches, and weighs 0.9 pounds empty. The body is 18/8 stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation and BPA-free components. Thermos states hot retention of 18 hours and cold retention of 24 hours; in independent testing the bottle led the field, though testers report contents warm rather than hot by the end of a full day. A twist-and-pour stopper lets liquid out without removing the entire lid, and the cap seals leak-proof when closed; the cup lid and pour-through stopper are convenient in the field, but the plastic handle and stopper are less robust than the steel body. The exterior stays sweat-proof and fits most vehicle cup holders, though the wide ridged body is harder to fit into a small bag. Included accessories: a serving cup built into the lid. Maintenance involves washing the body on the top rack of a dishwasher only, or by hand, and drying the stopper threads and cup separately before reassembly. The bottle is offered in several exterior colours, and the same stopper and cup design carries across the sizes in the line.
