The Honeywell HCM-350 is an evaporative cool mist humidifier for medium rooms. It measures 17.9 by 9.37 by 11.4 inches, weighs 8.36 pounds, and draws 60 watts from a 120-volt outlet. The 1.1-gallon tank runs up to 24 hours on the low setting per fill. A wicking filter draws water upward while a fan moves room air across it, so minerals stay in the filter rather than entering the air. Honeywell states that the design inhibits up to 99.99 percent of mold growth on the filter surface. Three speed settings adjust both moisture output and sound level. The tank has a wide opening for filling, lifts out of the base, and is dishwasher safe. Included accessories: one Replacement Filter A wicking filter. Maintenance involves rinsing the tank and base, replacing the wicking filter on a seasonal schedule, and drying the housing between periods of use.
