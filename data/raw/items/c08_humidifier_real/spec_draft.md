The Honeywell HCM-350 is an evaporative cool mist humidifier for medium rooms. It measures 17.9 by 9.37 by 11.4 inches, weighs 8.36 pounds, and draws 60 watts from a 120-volt, 60-hertz outlet. The 1.1-gallon tank runs up to 24 hours on the low setting per fill and is refilled about every day. A wicking filter draws water upward while a fan moves room air across it, so minerals stay in the filter and no mineral dust reaches the air; output falls as the room approaches its own balance. Mist leaves the unit cool, with no warm mist setting fitted. The unit raises room humidity to 45 percent in 34 minutes, and fan noise is louder than most evaporative units its size. The design inhibits up to 99.99 percent of mould growth on the filter surface, though the wick can still grow mould if it never dries between uses. The housing takes Replacement Filter A wicking filters only, and the wick is a recurring seasonal purchase. A single three-speed dial adjusts moisture output and sound level; there is no humidistat and no humidity target, so the unit does not switch itself off at a set level. The tank has a 3.5 inch opening wide enough to admit a hand for scrubbing, lifts out of the base, and is dishwasher safe along with the cover. The tank body is break resistant. The unit carries a three year limited warranty. Included accessories: one Replacement Filter A wicking filter. Maintenance involves rinsing the tank and base, replacing the wicking filter seasonally, and drying the housing between periods of use. The tank sits inverted in the base, its cover unscrews for filling at a tap, and the fan draws room air across the wick from one side. The fan and housing arrangement is a QuietCare design.
