The Orvia Petrel is a robot vacuum for household floors. The unit is 12.9 inches wide and 3.5 inches high and weighs 6.8 pounds. A lithium ion battery gives about 90 minutes of runtime and recharges in roughly two hours at the dock. The onboard dustbin holds 0.5 liters and sits behind a hinged door with a washable filter. Gyroscope navigation moves the vacuum in parallel passes and resumes by dead reckoning after a recharge; the unit does not store floor plans between runs. A single side brush sweeps debris toward a bristle main brush. The charging dock supplies power only and has no dust disposal. A companion app sets schedules and suction level. Included accessories: the charging dock, a spare filter, and a cleaning tool. Maintenance involves emptying the bin, rinsing the filter, and clearing hair from the brush.
