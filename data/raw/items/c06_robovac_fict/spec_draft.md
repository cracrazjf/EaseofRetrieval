The Orvia Petrel is a robot vacuum for household floors. The unit measures 12.9 inches across and 3.5 inches high and weighs 6.8 pounds, and it passes under furniture that clears that height. A lithium ion battery gives 90 minutes of runtime per charge, which covers a limited floor area; the robot then returns to the charging dock on its own and takes two hours to reach a full charge. The dustbin holds 0.5 litres and lifts out through a hinged door on the top of the robot. It is emptied by hand after every run, and it fills within a few rooms. A washable filter sits behind the bin door and goes back in once it has dried after rinsing. Gyroscope navigation moves the vacuum in parallel passes and resumes by dead reckoning. The unit stores no floor plans between runs, so no single room can be selected for cleaning, and it forgets the layout from one run to the next. There is no camera, so objects left on the floor are not recognised. A single side brush sweeps debris in from along the wall toward a bristle main brush, which collects hair over time, and the far edge of a run is left unswept. It climbs thresholds up to 18 millimetres and stops at anything higher. The charging dock supplies power only and has no dust disposal. A companion app sets schedules and suction level and reports the battery level and a run history; the vacuum joins a 2.4 gigahertz Wi-Fi network. There is no voice assistant control, so no run can be started by voice. Sound during a run is rated at 62 decibels. Included accessories: the charging dock, a spare filter, and a cleaning tool. Maintenance involves emptying the bin, rinsing the filter, and clearing hair from the brush.
