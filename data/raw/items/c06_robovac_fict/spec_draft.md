The Orvia Petrel is a robot vacuum for household floors. The unit is 12.9 inches wide and 3.5 inches high and weighs 6.8 pounds. A lithium ion battery gives about 90 minutes of runtime and recharges in roughly two hours at the dock, which the robot returns to on its own. The onboard dustbin holds 0.5 litres and sits behind a hinged door with a washable filter; it is emptied by hand after each run. Gyroscope navigation moves the vacuum in parallel passes and resumes by dead reckoning after a recharge. The unit stores no floor plans between runs, so no single room can be selected for cleaning. A single side brush sweeps debris toward a bristle main brush, which collects hair over time. It climbs thresholds up to 18 millimetres; anything higher stops it. The charging dock supplies power only and has no dust disposal. A companion app sets schedules and suction level. Included accessories: the charging dock, a spare filter, and a cleaning tool. Maintenance involves emptying the bin, rinsing the filter, and clearing hair from the brush. The washable filter sits behind the bin door and goes back in once it has dried fully after rinsing.
