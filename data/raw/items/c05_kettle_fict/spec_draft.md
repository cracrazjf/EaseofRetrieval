The Tessro Kettle is a gooseneck electric kettle for pour-over brewing. It measures 11.4 by 7.1 by 8.0 inches and weighs 1300 grams including its base. The kettle holds 40 ounces, or 1.2 liters, and runs on 110- to 120-volt power at 1200 watts. The body is 304 stainless steel with a BPA-free handle and lid pull. Five preset buttons select temperatures from 140 to 212 degrees Fahrenheit, shown on a backlit segment display. A hold mode keeps the selected temperature for thirty minutes, and a boil-dry cutoff shuts the element off when the kettle is empty. The gooseneck spout directs the stream during pouring. The power cord measures three feet. Included accessories: the kettle body and its powered base. Maintenance involves periodic descaling of the stainless interior, wiping the exterior after each use, and keeping the base contacts dry and free of mineral residue.
