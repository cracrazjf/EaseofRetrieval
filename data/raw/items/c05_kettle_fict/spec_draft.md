The Tessro Kettle is a gooseneck electric kettle for pour-over brewing. It measures 290 by 180 by 203 millimetres, weighs 1300 grams including its base, and holds 40 ounces, or 1.2 litres. It runs on 110- to 120-volt power at 1200 watts. The body is 304 stainless steel with a BPA-free handle and lid pull. Five preset buttons select temperatures from 140 to 212 degrees Fahrenheit, shown on a backlit segment display; temperature cannot be set to a specific degree between those presets, and there is no altitude adjustment. A hold mode keeps the selected temperature for thirty minutes and cannot be extended, and the kettle has no scheduling function and no brew stopwatch. A boil-dry cutoff shuts the element off when the kettle runs empty. The gooseneck spout pours deliberately slowly to direct the stream. The cord measures three feet, and the powered base adds its own counter footprint. Included accessories: the kettle body and its powered base. Maintenance involves periodic descaling of the stainless interior, wiping the exterior after use, and keeping the base contacts free of mineral residue. The lid lifts off for filling, and the handle stays cool while the element runs at full power.
