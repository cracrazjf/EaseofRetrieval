The Oral-B Pro 1000 is a rechargeable electric toothbrush. The round head oscillates, rotates, and pulsates against the tooth surface, wrapping around each tooth in turn rather than sweeping across several at once, and Oral-B states that this removes 300 percent more plaque along the gumline than a manual brush. Independent testing finds the plaque removal effective, though the vibration runs strong for sensitive gums. A pressure sensor stops the pulsation when the user presses too hard, and a timer in the handle marks off two minutes; reviewers note that a pressure sensor and timer are uncommon at entry level. Independent measurement places running noise at about 77 decibels close to the ear. The handle accepts seven Oral-B replacement head types, sold in packs that fit the same handle. It recharges on a charging stand included in the box, and charging needs the supplied stand rather than a USB cable. Reviewers measure ten to twelve days of use per charge, and a full recharge takes about 22 hours, the better part of a day; the cell chemistry is not stated by the maker. There is no smartphone connectivity, and the handle connects to no app or phone. The product carries a two year warranty. Included accessories: one CrossAction brush head, which ships with the handle, and the charging stand; no travel case is included. Maintenance involves rinsing the head and handle under a running tap, standing both to dry between uses, and replacing heads as the bristles splay outward from the centre of the brush face. The stand charges the handle by induction through the plastic, with no exposed contacts on either part, and holds the handle upright on the counter while it charges. The brush head clips onto a shaft at the top of the handle and pulls off by hand.
