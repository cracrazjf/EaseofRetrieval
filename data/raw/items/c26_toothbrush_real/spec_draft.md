The Oral-B Pro 1000 is a rechargeable electric toothbrush. The round head oscillates, rotates, and pulsates against the tooth surface, and Oral-B states that this removes 300 percent more plaque along the gumline than a manual brush. A pressure sensor stops the pulsation when the user presses too hard, and a timer in the handle signals when two minutes have elapsed. The handle accepts seven Oral-B replacement head types, so the brushing surface can be changed without replacing the handle. The handle recharges on an included stand. The product carries a two year warranty. Included accessories: one CrossAction brush head and the charging stand. Maintenance involves rinsing the head and the handle after each use, separating the two so both dry upright on the stand, wiping the metal drive shaft, and replacing the brush head once the bristles begin to splay outward from the centre.
