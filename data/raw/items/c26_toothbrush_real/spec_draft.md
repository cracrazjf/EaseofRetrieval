The Oral-B Pro 1000 is a rechargeable electric toothbrush. The round head oscillates, rotates, and pulsates against the tooth surface, wrapping around each tooth, and Oral-B states that this removes 300 percent more plaque along the gumline than a manual brush; independent testing finds plaque removal effective, though the vibration is strong for sensitive gums. A pressure sensor stops the pulsation when the user presses too hard, and a timer in the handle signals when two minutes have elapsed; that sensor and quadrant timer are uncommon at entry level, and the feature set is unusual at this price point. Independent measurement places running noise at about 77 decibels at close range. The handle accepts seven Oral-B replacement head types and recharges on an included stand, running ten to twelve days per charge with a recharge cycle of about a day; the cell chemistry is not stated by the maker. The product carries a two year warranty. Included accessories: one CrossAction brush head and the charging stand. Maintenance involves rinsing the head and handle and replacing the head as the bristles splay. The round head oscillates, rotates, and pulsates through each brushing stroke, and heads are replaced as the bristles splay outward from the centre.
