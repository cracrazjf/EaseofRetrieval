Oral-B Pro 1000 Review: The Best No-Frills Electric Toothbrush

Published Oct 1, 2025
|
Reviewed by Dr. Ben Carter, DMD, MD
|
Testing 4 weeks
|
Evidence Strong Clinical Evidence
PEER REVIEWED

The Oral-B Pro 1000 is a workhorse. It forgoes connectivity, multiple modes, and fancy displays for two things: an effective 3D cleaning action (oscillating, rotating, and pulsating) and an affordable price. Our review confirms that its single 'Daily Clean' mode is sufficient for most users. The two most critical features for good oral hygiene are present: a two-minute quadrant timer that pulses every 30 seconds, and a visible pressure sensor that stops pulsations when you press too hard. For users looking to upgrade from a manual toothbrush, the Pro 1000 provides the most significant clinical benefits at the lowest cost.
