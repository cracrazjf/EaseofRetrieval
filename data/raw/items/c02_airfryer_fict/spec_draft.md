The Farnholt A5 is a countertop air fryer for home use. It holds a 5.5-quart round basket, measures 11.4 by 13.2 by 12.8 inches, and weighs 11.6 pounds; that footprint takes a noticeable stretch of counter depth. The unit runs on 120-volt household power at 1600 watts, a draw shared with anything else on the same circuit. Temperature is adjustable from 180 to 400 degrees Fahrenheit and the timer covers one to sixty minutes, with no setting beyond that hour. A dial and button panel selects eight presets: fries, chicken, steak, fish, vegetables, bake, reheat, and keep warm, with manual time and temperature entry for anything else. The panel has no wireless or app connection, so the unit cannot be started or scheduled remotely, and nothing prompts the cook to shake the basket partway through. The round basket sits inside a square drawer, leaving the corners unused, and cooks in a single layer, so larger quantities are done in batches. The drawer has no viewing window. The basket and crisper plate carry a nonstick coating and are dishwasher safe. Included accessories: a recipe booklet, a quick start guide, and a user manual.
