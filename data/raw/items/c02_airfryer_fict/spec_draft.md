The Farnholt A5 is a countertop air fryer for home use. It holds a 5.5-quart round basket and measures 11.4 by 13.2 by 12.8 inches, weighing 11.6 pounds. The unit runs on 120-volt household power at 1600 watts. Temperature is adjustable from 180 to 400 degrees Fahrenheit, and the timer covers one to sixty minutes. A dial and button panel selects eight presets: fries, chicken, steak, fish, vegetables, bake, reheat, and keep warm; manual time and temperature entry is available for other foods. The panel operates without any wireless or app connection. The basket and crisper plate carry a nonstick coating and are dishwasher safe. Included accessories: a recipe booklet, a quick start guide, and a user manual. Maintenance involves washing the basket and crisper plate after each use, wiping the interior heating chamber once it has cooled, and checking the rear air intake for obstructions.
