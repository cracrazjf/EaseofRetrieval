The Farnholt A5 is a countertop air fryer for home use. It holds a 5.5-quart, 5.2-litre round basket, measures 11.4 by 13.2 by 12.8 inches, or 29 by 34 by 33 centimetres, and weighs 11.6 pounds, which is 5.3 kilograms; the footprint of about 29 by 34 centimetres occupies that much counter depth. The unit runs on AC 120-volt, 60-hertz household power at a rated 1600 watts, a draw shared with anything else on the same kitchen circuit. Temperature is adjustable from 180 to 400 degrees Fahrenheit, equal to 82 to 204 Celsius, and the timer covers one minute to sixty minutes. A dial and button panel selects eight presets: fries, chicken, steak, fish, vegetables, bake, reheat, and keep warm; time and temperature are entered by hand for anything outside those eight, so fewer presets means more manual entry. The panel has no wireless control, so there is no remote start and no scheduling from a phone, and nothing reminds the cook to shake the basket mid-cycle. The round basket sits inside a square drawer and leaves the corners unused. The single basket cooks one layer at a time, so larger quantities go in batches. The drawer has no viewing window. The basket releases from the drawer with a button on the handle, and the drawer handle stays cool to the touch during cooking. The removable crisper plate raises food for airflow underneath. The nonstick basket and crisper plate are dishwasher safe. Included accessories: a recipe booklet, a quick start guide, and a user manual. Maintenance involves washing the basket and the crisper plate after use and wiping the interior once it has cooled. The drawer slides out from the front and reseats with a click. A power indicator on the panel shows when the element is drawing current. The basket and the crisper plate are the only parts that come away for washing, and neither needs tools.
