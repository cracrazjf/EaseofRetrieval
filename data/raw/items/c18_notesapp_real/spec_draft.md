Notion is a workspace application in which pages and databases are assembled from blocks that can be nested, linked, and reused. Content is organised into shared workspaces with per-page permissions and guest access. Four plans are offered: free, Plus, Business, and Enterprise. The free plan gives individuals unlimited blocks, with limits applied to multi-member workspaces, a 5-megabyte cap on file uploads, ten guests, and seven days of page history. AI features are trial-limited on the free and Plus plans and included on Business and Enterprise. Pages export as PDF, HTML, or Markdown, and databases export as CSV. Pages marked available offline can be read and edited on the desktop and mobile clients, though new pages, uploads, AI, and formula recalculation require a connection. Clients exist for the web, for macOS and Windows on the desktop, and for iOS and Android on mobile, with content syncing between them automatically when connected.
