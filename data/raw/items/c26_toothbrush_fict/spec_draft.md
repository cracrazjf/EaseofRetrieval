The Dovrell 230 is a rechargeable electric toothbrush. The head vibrates at sonic frequency against the tooth surface rather than rotating, a motion that feels different from oscillating rotation to anyone used to it. A pressure sensor reduces output when the user presses too hard against the gums. A timer runs for two minutes with a pause every thirty seconds to pace movement between quadrants of the mouth. The handle accepts three head shapes from one maker and no others. Charging is over a USB-C port in the base of the handle, so the charging cable travels with the brush and there is no charging stand for a bathroom counter; a full charge lasts about three weeks of twice-daily use. Included accessories: one brush head supplied in the box, a hard travel case that adds bulk to a packed bag, and a USB-C cable. The product carries a two year warranty. Maintenance involves rinsing the head and handle after each use, drying both upright, and replacing the head once the bristles begin to splay outward from the centre of the brush face. The two minute timer pauses every thirty seconds so the brush moves between quadrants of the mouth on an even schedule throughout the session.
