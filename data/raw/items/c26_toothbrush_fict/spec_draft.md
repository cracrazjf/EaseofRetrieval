The Dovrell 230 is a rechargeable electric toothbrush. The head vibrates at sonic frequency against the tooth surface rather than rotating, and a pressure sensor reduces output when the user presses too hard against the gums. A timer runs for two minutes with a pause every thirty seconds to pace movement between quadrants of the mouth. The handle accepts three head shapes from the same maker. Charging is over a USB-C port in the base of the handle, and a full charge lasts about three weeks of twice-daily use. Included accessories: one brush head, a hard travel case, and a USB-C cable. The product carries a two year warranty. Maintenance involves rinsing the head and handle after each use, drying both upright, and replacing the head once the bristles begin to splay outward from the centre of the brush face.
