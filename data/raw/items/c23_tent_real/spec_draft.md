The MSR Hubba Hubba NX is a freestanding three-season backpacking tent for two people. Minimum weight is 3 pounds 7 ounces and packaged weight is 3 pounds 13 ounces, packing to 18 by 6 inches. The floor measures 84 by 50 inches, giving 29 square feet, and interior peak height is 39 inches. Two doors open onto two vestibules totalling 17.5 square feet, and each door carries a rain gutter. The rainfly and canopy use 20 denier ripstop nylon, the fly coated to 1200 millimetres with polyurethane and silicone, and the canopy fitted with micro-mesh panels. The floor is 30 denier ripstop nylon coated to 3000 millimetres. A unified hub and pole assembly in DAC Featherlite NFL aluminium sets the shape. Included accessories: stakes, guylines, and a stuff sack. A separate footprint is available for the model. Maintenance involves drying the fly and floor fully before storage and keeping the pole sections clean at the joints.
