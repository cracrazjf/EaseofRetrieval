The MSR Hubba Hubba NX is a freestanding three-season backpacking tent for two people, light for a freestanding double-wall design. Minimum weight is 3 pounds 7 ounces and packaged weight 3 pounds 13 ounces, packing to 18 by 6 inches. The floor measures 84 by 50 inches, giving 29 square feet, and interior peak height is 39 inches; near-vertical walls give usable interior space. Two doors open onto two vestibules totalling 17.5 square feet, easing access, and each door carries a rain gutter. The rainfly and canopy use 20 denier ripstop nylon, the fly coated to 1200 millimetres with polyurethane and silicone, and the canopy fitted with micro-mesh panels. The floor is 30 denier ripstop nylon coated to 3000 millimetres, thin enough that reviewers recommend a separately purchased footprint. A unified hub and pole assembly in DAC Featherlite NFL aluminium sets the shape, and the symmetrical pole layout makes the pitch quick, though reviewers find the rainfly attachment unintuitive. Included accessories: stakes, guylines, and a stuff sack. The tent stands on hard ground without stakes. Maintenance involves drying the fly and floor fully before storage and keeping the pole sections clean at the joints where they seat together.
