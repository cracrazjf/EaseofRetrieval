The MSR Hubba Hubba NX is a freestanding three-season backpacking tent for two people, and it is light for a freestanding double-wall tent. Minimum weight is 3 pounds 7 ounces and packaged weight 3 pounds 13 ounces, coming to nearly four pounds; the minimum weight figure covers canopy, rainfly, and poles and does not include the stakes, the guylines, or the stuff sack. It packs down to 18 by 6 inches. The floor measures 84 by 50 inches, giving 29 square feet that takes two sleeping pads, though 50 inches across is tight for two adults. Interior peak height is 39 inches, and near-vertical walls make the interior usable rather than merely enterable; 39 inches of headroom keeps taller campers hunched. Two doors mean neither occupant climbs over the other, each carries a rain gutter that sheds water away, and the two vestibules total 17.5 square feet for gear. The rainfly is 20 denier ripstop nylon coated to 1200 millimetres with polyurethane and silicone, a coating that is modest for sustained rain. The canopy is 20 denier ripstop nylon with micro-mesh panels. The floor is 30 denier ripstop nylon coated to 3000 millimetres, thin enough to need a footprint, which is bought separately. A unified hub and pole assembly in DAC Featherlite NFL aluminium comes as one piece, and its symmetrical layout means the poles go up the same way every time, though getting the rainfly on the right way is not obvious. The tent stands on hard ground without a single stake. Included accessories: stakes, guylines, and a stuff sack; no footprint and no gear loft come with the tent. Maintenance involves drying the fly and floor before storage and keeping the pole joints clean. The canopy zips shut against insects, and the fly stakes out at the corners and along the guyline points.
