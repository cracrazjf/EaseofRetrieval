The Levoit Core 300 is a portable air purifier for single rooms. The cylindrical unit measures 8.7 by 8.7 by 14.2 inches, weighs 7.9 pounds, and draws 56 watts from a 120-volt outlet; newer models in the line do the same work on less power. Levoit rates clean air delivery at 143 cubic feet per minute, covering 1066 square feet at one air change per hour or 222 square feet at 4.8 air changes. Independent testing measured delivery between 126 and 154 cubic feet per minute depending on particle type. Air passes through a pre-filter, a main filter, and a thin activated carbon layer that limits heavy odour removal; the pre-filter clogs unless it is vacuumed regularly. Rated sound runs from 24 to 54.5 decibels, sleep mode sitting at 24 decibels at the bottom of that range, and independent measurement places the highest fan speed at 50 to 65 dBA. A touch panel sets fan speed and sleep mode and starts a timer of two, four, six, or eight hours, and a filter replacement indicator lights when the filter is spent. There is no air quality sensor and no auto mode, so the fan speed never changes on its own, and no app control or remote is offered. Replacement filters cost little and are widely stocked. The unit is ozone free, CARB compliant, and FCC and ETL listed. At 14.2 inches tall it occupies a side table or a floor corner, and the 8.7 inch base takes up less than nine inches square. Levoit lists the filter's expected life at six to eight months in typical use. Included accessories: a three-stage filter installed at the factory, a user manual, and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves vacuuming the pre-filter and replacing the filter when the panel indicates.
