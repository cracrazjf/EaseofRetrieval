The Levoit Core 300 is a portable air purifier for single rooms. The cylindrical unit measures 8.7 by 8.7 by 14.2 inches, or 22 by 22 by 36 centimetres, weighs 7.9 pounds, which is 3.6 kilograms, and draws 56 watts from a 120-volt outlet; newer models in the line do the same work on less power. Rated clean air delivery is 143 cubic feet per minute, covering 1066 square feet at one air change per hour or 222 square feet at 4.8 air changes. Delivery runs between 126 and 154 cubic feet per minute depending on particle type. Air passes through a pre-filter, a main filter, and a thin activated carbon layer that limits heavy odour removal; the pre-filter clogs unless it is vacuumed regularly. The unit does not use ultraviolet light or ionisation, and the main filter cannot be washed; it is replaced rather than cleaned. Rated sound runs from 24 to 54.5 decibels, sleep mode sitting at 24 decibels at the bottom of that range, and the highest fan speed runs 50 to 65 dBA. A touch panel sets fan speed and sleep mode and starts a timer of two, four, six, or eight hours, and a filter replacement indicator lights when the filter is spent. There is no air quality sensor and no auto mode, so the fan speed never changes on its own, and no app control or remote is offered. Replacement filters cost little and are widely stocked. The unit is ozone free, CARB compliant, and FCC and ETL listed. At 14.2 inches tall it occupies a side table or a floor corner. Expected filter life is six to eight months in typical use. Included accessories: a three-stage filter installed at the factory, a user manual, and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves vacuuming the pre-filter and replacing the filter when the panel indicates.
