The Levoit Core 300 is a portable air purifier for single rooms. The cylindrical unit measures 8.7 by 8.7 by 14.2 inches, weighs 7.9 pounds, and draws 56 watts from a 120-volt outlet, more than newer models in the line use for the same work. Levoit rates clean air delivery at 143 cubic feet per minute, covering 1066 square feet at one air change per hour or 222 square feet at 4.8 air changes, which is strong particle removal for the size class. Air passes through a pre-filter, a main filter, and a thin activated carbon layer that limits heavy odour removal. Rated sound runs from 24 to 54.5 decibels, and independent measurement places the highest fan speed at 50 to 65 dBA. A touch panel sets fan speed, a timer at two, four, six, or eight hours, and sleep mode, and a filter indicator signals replacement; there is no air quality sensor, no auto mode, and no app control. Replacement filters are inexpensive and widely stocked. The unit is ozone free, CARB compliant, and FCC and ETL listed. Included accessories: a three-stage filter installed at the factory, a user manual, and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves vacuuming the pre-filter.
