The Levoit Core 300 is a portable air purifier for single rooms. The cylindrical unit measures 8.7 by 8.7 by 14.2 inches and weighs 7.9 pounds. It draws 56 watts from a 120-volt outlet. Levoit rates the clean air delivery rate at 143 cubic feet per minute, covering 1066 square feet at one air change per hour or 222 square feet at 4.8 air changes. Air passes through three stages: a pre-filter, a main filter, and an activated carbon layer. Measured sound output ranges from 24 to 54.5 decibels across fan speeds. A touch panel sets fan speed, a timer at two, four, six, or eight hours, and sleep mode, and a filter indicator signals replacement. Included accessories: a filter installed at the factory, a user manual, and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves vacuuming the pre-filter and replacing the filter.
