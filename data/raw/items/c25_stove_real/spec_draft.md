The SOTO WindMaster is a canister-mounted camping stove. With its four-arm pot support fitted it weighs 2.3 ounces, or 67 grams, a weight suited to long-distance carrying; the burner alone weighs 2.12 ounces. Stowed with the support it measures 1.9 by 3.0 by 1.7 inches, which packs bulkier than competing canister stoves because those support arms detach; they must be fitted each use and are easily lost. Output is rated at 2800 kilocalories per hour, or 3260 watts and 11,000 British thermal units, and SOTO states two cups of water reach a boil in under two and a half minutes in wind, though independent times range from 2:27 to 4:02 depending on protocol. One eight-ounce canister runs about ninety minutes. The burner accepts butane, isobutane, and propane mixes but not pure propane. A concave burner head sits close under the pot and holds the flame in wind, a micro regulator valve holds output as canisters cool, and flame control is fine enough for simmering. The pot supports stay stable under a full pot up to two litres. Included accessories: the four-arm support and a stuff sack. A built-in piezo igniter sits on the burner post, the body is die-cast aluminium, and the supports take pots holding up to 1.5 litres of liquid.
