The SOTO WindMaster is a canister-mounted camping stove. With its four-arm pot support fitted it weighs 2.3 ounces, or 67 grams; the burner alone weighs 2.12 ounces. Stowed with the support it measures 1.9 by 3.0 by 1.7 inches. Output is rated at 2800 kilocalories per hour, equivalent to 3260 watts or 11,000 British thermal units, and SOTO states that two cups of water reach a boil in under two and a half minutes even in wind. One eight-ounce canister runs about an hour and a half. The burner accepts butane, isobutane, and propane mixes but not pure propane. A concave burner head sits close under the pot, a micro regulator valve holds output steady, and a piezo igniter is built into the burner post. The die-cast aluminium body supports pots up to two litres holding 1.5 litres of liquid. Included accessories: the four-arm pot support and a stuff sack.
