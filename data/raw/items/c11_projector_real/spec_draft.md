The XGIMI MoGo 2 is a portable projector for home use. It measures 161 by 119 by 108 millimetres and weighs 1.1 kilograms, light enough to carry in a bag. The DLP light engine uses a 0.23-inch digital micromirror device to produce a 1280 by 720 image at 400 ISO lumens, covering 90 percent of the DCI-P3 range at D65. The 720p resolution softens with detailed content, and 400 ISO lumens needs a darkened room. Throw ratio is 1.2 to 1, so a large picture needs real distance, and the image spans 40 to 200 inches. Auto focus, automatic keystone correction, screen alignment, and obstacle avoidance run each time the projector is moved, and the sequence completes without manual adjustment. Two 8-watt speakers carry Dolby Audio decoding and play louder than the cabinet size suggests. Wired connections are one HDMI 2.0 input, a USB-C port, a USB-A port, and a 3.5-millimetre auxiliary jack; there is no second HDMI input, so two sources are swapped by cable. Wireless connections are dual-band Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.0. The Android TV 11 platform runs on 2 gigabytes of memory and 16 gigabytes of storage. The projector draws 65 watts from mains power and has no internal battery, so it runs from an outlet or from a power bank supplying at least 65 watts. The USB-C port and the USB-A port take storage and peripherals, and the 3.5-millimetre jack feeds a wired speaker or headphones when the internal pair is not wanted. Operating noise stays under 30 decibels. Included accessories: a power adapter and a remote control; no carrying case, tripod, or screen is supplied. Maintenance involves keeping the lens and the intake vents clear of dust, since a blocked intake raises the operating temperature.
