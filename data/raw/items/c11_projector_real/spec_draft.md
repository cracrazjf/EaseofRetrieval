The XGIMI MoGo 2 is a portable projector for home use. It measures 161 by 119 by 108 millimetres and weighs 1.1 kilograms. The DLP light engine uses a 0.23-inch device to produce a 1280 by 720 image at 400 ISO lumens, covering 90 percent of the DCI-P3 range at D65; that resolution softens with detailed content, and 400 lumens needs a darkened room. Throw ratio is 1.2 to 1, giving an image between 40 and 200 inches. Auto focus, keystone correction, screen alignment, and obstacle avoidance run without manual adjustment when the projector is moved. Two 8-watt speakers carry Dolby Audio and are louder than the size suggests. Connections include one HDMI 2.0 input, USB-C, USB-A, and a 3.5-millimetre jack, with dual-band Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.0. Android TV 11 runs on 2 gigabytes of memory and 16 gigabytes of storage. The unit draws 65 watts from mains power and has no internal battery, so it runs from an outlet or a 65-watt power bank. Operating noise stays under 30 decibels. Included accessories: a power adapter and a remote control.
