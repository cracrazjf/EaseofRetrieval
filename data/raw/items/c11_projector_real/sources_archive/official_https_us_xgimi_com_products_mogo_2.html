Quick Look
Enormous Fun Begins Here

MoGo 2 was designed for fun-filled home entertainment whenever you need it. Featuring a bright 400 ISO lumens, the highest in its class, D65 color temperature standard used in Hollywood, premier built-in surround sound speakers, and our ISA 1.0 technology, MoGo 2 delivers a professional visual experience in a small build but in a big way!

XGIMI Intelligent Screen Adaption Technology

XGIMI's industry-leading Intelligent Screen Adaption (ISA) technology eliminates complex projector setups. Featuring Auto Keystone Correction, Auto Focus, Intelligent Screen Alignment, and Intelligent Obstacle Avoidance, MoGo 2 provides the automated and uninterrupted viewing experience you deserve.

Auto Keystone Correction

Position MoGo 2 directly in front of the wall/screen or at vertical/horizontal +/-40° angles and watch as it automatically adjusts its projected display to the ideal square image.

Auto Focus

Auto Focus keeps your display clear and your viewing experience uninterrupted.

Intelligent Obstacle Avoidance

MoGo 2 automatically scales its display around sockets, picture frames, and plants.

Intelligent Screen Alignment

Setup is fast and easy. MoGo 2 automatically matches its image with your screen in seconds!

I love this projector. I previously had one at my old house which was a great buy, after I saw one that my friend had (and still has).
