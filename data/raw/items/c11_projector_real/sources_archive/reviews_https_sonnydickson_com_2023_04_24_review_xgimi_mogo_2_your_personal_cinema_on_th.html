Review: XGIMI MoGo 2: Your Personal Cinema on the Go

Brian Douek April 24, 2023

What distinguishes the XGIMI MoGo 2 from other portable projectors? In my opinion, it’s the intelligent screen adaptation, auto focus, auto keystone correction, and intelligent obstacle avoidance features. Let me walk you through my personal experience with this incredibly innovative projector.

Let’s dive right in. The XGIMI MoGo 2’s intelligent screen features are truly impressive. I was blown away by the speed and accuracy of the adjustments it can make. For instance, even if the device is not placed perfectly straight, it can still project a clear and undistorted image. With these intelligent features, users can always expect a perfectly aligned and focused image. Additionally, the device allows for manual adjustments, which is extremely helpful when adjusting the picture to fit a specific screen size. It is possible to move each corner of the picture to the desired location on the screen, making it even more accurate to your viewing angle. I love that adjusting the device on the fly is fast and effortless.

The MoGo 2 also has a high brightness of 400 ISO lumens, ensuring a clear quality image even in brightly-lit environments. Living in Florida, where sunny days are common, I had little expectation of being able to use a projector indoors during the day. However, the MoGo 2 exceeded my expectations by providing a pretty clear and vibrant picture despite the brightness of the living room. The compact size of the device also makes it easy to setup anywhere.

Intelligent and Adaptable Projection

The XGIMI MoGo 2 features an HD 720P Intelligent Screen Adaption (ISA) display, which makes it ideal for enjoying movies, sports games, or any other content on a large screen without having to worry about a slanted or crooked image. While it may not be a 1080p projector, the DLP display chip, along with a 0.23” DMD and HDR10 support, delivers sharp and vibrant colors. Additionally, the high light transmission coated lens guarantees a bright and clear image.

The XGIMI MoGo 2 makes it simple to adjust the screen size to fit any room or space with a throw ratio of 1.2:1 and a projection range of 40”-200”. Its auto focus feature, auto keystone correction, and intelligent screen alignment ensure an effortless setup and the perfect picture every time. Even better, the projector comes equipped with an intelligent obstacle avoidance feature that automatically moves the image away from any obstructions in its path. This is especially convenient when you’re short on time and need to quickly set up the projector for the kids to watch something. The MoGo 2 will promptly adjust away from whatever may be in view and project to a flat surface it detects.

How is the Audio?

While the XGIMI MoGo 2 may not produce SoundBar-level audio quality, it still packs a punch with its dual 8W speakers that are surprisingly powerful for its compact size. The sound is well-balanced and fits perfectly with the projector itself, delivering clear and vibrant audio. With support for Dolby Audio, Dolby Digital (DD), and Dolby Digital Plus (DD+), you can enjoy an immersive and high-quality audio experience that brings your content to life like never before. Whether you’re watching your favorite movie or playing a video game, the MoGo 2’s audio capabilities will enhance your overall experience. It’s like having a mini home theater right in your pocket! In comparison to other portable projectors on the market, the MoGo 2 produces a sound that can be likened to an updated Amazon Echo, delivering impressive highs and lows that will make you feel like you’re right in the middle of the action.

Smooth and Efficient Performance

The XGIMI MoGo 2 comes with Android TV™ 11.0 pre-installed, making it effortless to navigate and utilize, with instant access to your preferred streaming applications such as Netflix, Hulu, and Amazon Prime Video. Additionally, with built-in Chromecast functionality, you can easily mirror your phone or laptop screen and enjoy your desired content on a larger display. Another notable aspect of this projector is the remote, which has an easy-to-use design and operates via Bluetooth instead of infrared technology. This means that users can adjust the settings and control the device from any angle, without having to aim the remote at the projector.

Easily Connect with Your Devices

The XGIMI MoGo 2 is not only a powerful projector but also incredibly versatile. With multiple input and output ports, including USB type-C, USB type-A, and HDMI 2.0, you can easily connect it to all your favorite devices. Whether you want to stream content from your phone, tablet, or laptop, the MoGo 2 has you covered. And with dual-band 2.4/5GHz Wifi and Bluetooth 5.0, connectivity is a breeze.

In addition to its connectivity options, the MoGo 2 boasts a low noise level of ＜30dB, making it perfect for any room or space. You can enjoy your favorite content without any distracting fan noise. And with a power dissipation of 65W and AC100-240V, 50/60Hz power, the MoGo 2 won’t consume too much energy while in use.

Despite its powerful features, the MoGo 2 remains compact and portable. Weighing in at only 1.1kg (2.42lb) and measuring 161 × 119 × 108mm (6.33 × 4.68 × 4.25 Inch), it’s easy to take with you wherever you go. So whether you’re having a movie night with friends, presenting at work, or just need a bigger screen on the go, the MoGo 2 is a great solution.

Pros:

  • High-quality HD projection
  • Portable and lightweight
  • Powerful Speakers
  • Easy to set up and use
  • Multiple input and output ports

Cons:

  • Resolution is limited to 1280 × 720 pixels
  • Is not battery powered

Is the MoGo 2 the Ideal Choice?

The XGIMI MoGo 2 has an exceptional picture that is both crystal-clear and brightly illuminated. What’s even more impressive is that this comes at a very affordable price of just $399, which sets it apart from its competitors in the market. Moreover, its Intelligent Screen Features and portability make it a remarkable product that outshines other projectors out there.

It is worth noting that XGIMI offers several projector models, but the MoGo 2 is their most budget-friendly option, and its quality is still top-notch. While it lacks a battery, this drawback personally doesn’t deter me from using this product as the viewing experience is so exceptional. It is truly impressive that such a powerful projector is packed into such a compact size.

Overall, if you are searching for a projector that delivers a high-quality viewing experience without breaking the bank, the XGIMI MoGo 2 is the perfect solution. It is a budget-friendly projector that doesn’t compromise on quality, making it a fantastic investment for movie nights, sporting events, or streaming your favorite TV shows.
