XGIMI MoGo 2 Portable Projector Review

May 17, 2023 JabbaReview Smart Home Devices 0

XGIMI MoGo 2 Portable Projector

10

 10.0/10
XGIMI MoGo 2 Portable Projector

Founded in 2013, XGIMI has established itself as a leading provider of state-of-the-art projectors, offering consumers the opportunity to create immersive audio-visual experiences. With a commitment to excellence, XGIMI collaborates with renowned partners such as Google, Harman Kardon, and Texas Instruments to develop the best home and portable projectors in the market.

In this review, we will be exploring XGIMI’s latest offering, the MoGo 2. This portable projector boasts an impressive array of features, making it one of the most user-friendly projectors available today. Its compact design and portability enable users to easily carry it in a backpack and take it on trips, and with support for a 65W portable power bank, the MoGo 2 knows no limits in terms of its usage possibilities.

Pricing and Availability

In the United Kingdom, customers can purchase it for £359. The MoGo 2 Pro version is also available at a discounted price of £484 with the coupon, down from the RRP of £529.

XGIMI MoGo 2 Specifications and Key Features

The MoGo 2 boasts an impressive set of specifications and features that contribute to its outstanding performance:

Specifications:

  • 400 ISO lumens
  • 720p HD resolution
  • 90% DCI-P3 color gamut
  • D65 color temperature standard
  • HDR 10 enhanced color contrasts

Key Features:

Intelligent Screen Adaptation Technology (ISA 1.0): XGIMI’s Intelligent Screen Adaptation Technology ensures a seamless and hassle-free setup process. With this technology, users no longer need to worry about screen angle, alignment, or resolution, as the MoGo 2 takes care of everything automatically. Equipped with a small sensor on the front, the projector guarantees the best viewing angle, image quality, and picture without requiring any manual adjustments. This innovative technology simplifies the user experience and ensures optimal performance.

The ISA 1.0 also offers additional intelligent features, including:

  1. Intelligent Obstacle Avoidance: The projector automatically detects and adjusts for any obstacles in its path, ensuring an uninterrupted viewing experience.
  2. Intelligent Screen Alignment: The MoGo 2 precisely aligns the projected image with the screen, eliminating the need for manual adjustments.
  3. Automatic Keystone Correction: The projector automatically corrects any keystone distortion, providing a perfectly rectangular image even when projecting from an angle.
  4. Autofocus: The MoGo 2 instantly focuses on the content, eliminating the need for manual focus adjustments.

XGIMI Self-Developed Audio System: The MoGo 2 is equipped with a self-developed audio system that enhances the overall audio experience. With 2x8W speakers, the projector delivers immersive sound quality. Moreover, the MoGo 2 supports Dolby Audio and Dolby Digital (DD) and Dolby Digital Plus (DD+) technologies, further enhancing the audio performance. Whether you’re watching movies, playing games, or streaming music, the MoGo 2 ensures a rich and captivating audio experience.

Android TV 11.0 and Google Play:

One of the standout features of the MoGo 2 is its integration of Android TV 11.0, providing users with a wide range of entertainment options. With access to Google Play, users can download and install over 5,000 apps directly onto the projector, including popular streaming services like Disney+, HBO, Hulu, YouTube, and many more. The MoGo 2’s 16GB of internal storage offers ample space to store your favorite apps, allowing for easy access and seamless entertainment.

Compact Design for Portability:

The MoGo 2 boasts a compact design that sets it apart from other projectors in its category. With dimensions of 161 x 119 x 108mm (6.33 x 4.68 x 4.24 inches) and a weight of just 1.1kg (2.4 pounds), this projector is highly portable and can fit into most standard backpacks. Whether you’re going on a business trip, a family vacation, or a camping adventure, the MoGo 2 is your perfect companion, ensuring that you never miss out on your favorite movies, shows, or games while on the move.

Type C Protocol and Power Charge & Portable Power Bank Support:

The MoGo 2 features a versatile USB Type-C charging port, allowing for convenient power supply options. While the projector comes with a mains plug for traditional power sources, it can also be powered by a 65W or higher capacity portable power bank, providing flexibility and extended usage time. This feature makes the MoGo 2 an ideal choice for outdoor activities, such as backyard movie nights, camping trips, or impromptu gatherings with friends.

XGIMI MoGo 2 Packaging and Accessories:

The MoGo 2 arrives in a sleek white box with an attractive image of the projector on the front. XGIMI ensures the projector’s safety during shipping by utilizing extra foam padding inside the box, protecting it from any potential damage. Opening the box reveals the MoGo 2 projector itself, accompanied by a remote control with batteries, a mains charger, and all the necessary manuals. XGIMI has focused on providing essential accessories to keep the packaging minimal and clutter-free.

A minor consideration when unpacking the MoGo 2 is the orientation of the mains plug. Depending on your power leads, the prongs of the plug may occupy two plug spaces. While this is not a major issue, it’s worth noting to ensure compatibility or to purchase a suitable power lead if needed.

Examining the XGIMI MoGo 2 in Detail:

The MoGo 2’s design exudes elegance and sophistication. Its frosty grey finish and compact form factor make it visually appealing, leaving a lasting impression. The main lens, located at the top front of the projector, projects the video, while a small sensor at the bottom front enables the Auto Keystone feature, a standout characteristic of the MoGo 2. This feature ensures optimal viewing angles and image quality, automatically adjusting the projection to deliver the best possible visual experience.

At the top of the projector, you’ll find the XGIMI logo, a small power button, and a power indication LED. These minimalist design elements add a touch of simplicity and functionality to the overall aesthetics of the MoGo 2.

Turning the projector over, the bottom panel features important information and a mounting hole for the upcoming Freestyle Stand, set to be released in July 2023. Until then, the mounting hole allows users to attach the MoGo 2 to a tripod, offering additional versatility in terms of placement and positioning. Using a tripod not only provides stability but also expands the range of environments where the projector can be used.

Moving to the back of the XGIMI MoGo 2, we find the input ports that cater to various connectivity needs. These include a USB Type-C port, an HDMI 2.0 port, a USB 2.0 port, and an audio 3.5mm jack. These ports ensure compatibility with a wide range of devices, allowing seamless connectivity for gaming consoles, laptops, media players, and more. The inclusion of an audio jack is a thoughtful addition, enabling users to connect headphones directly to the projector and enjoy a personalized audio experience.

Just below the connectivity ports, you’ll notice the bass diaphragm, which offers a unique viewing experience by providing a glimpse of the speaker and its sound movement. While this feature doesn’t significantly impact performance, it adds a visually interesting element and contributes to the overall appeal of the MoGo 2.

Setup, Apps, and Storage:

Setting up the XGIMI MoGo 2 is a breeze. Upon turning on the projector, you’re greeted with a user-friendly welcome screen that allows you to select your preferred language. The setup process is quick and straightforward, taking only a few minutes before you can start enjoying the projector’s features.

With Android TV 11.0 and Google Play integration, the MoGo 2 provides access to an extensive library of apps, totaling over 5,000. This means that right out of the box, you can download and install apps like Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, and more. The convenience of having these apps directly on the projector eliminates the need for additional external devices, simplifying your entertainment setup.

The MoGo 2 offers 16GB of internal storage, providing ample space for app installations and downloads. After accounting for the pre-installed apps, you have approximately 10.4GB of available storage for your favorite apps, games, and multimedia content. This generous storage capacity ensures that you can personalize the projector with your preferred apps and have them readily accessible whenever you need them.

YouTube Usage and Chromecast:

Using YouTube on the XGIMI MoGo 2 is seamless and user-friendly, offering two convenient methods. First, you can download the YouTube app directly onto the projector, allowing for a standalone YouTube experience without the need for additional devices. With the app installed, you can sign in to your YouTube account and enjoy your favorite content with just a few clicks.

Alternatively, the MoGo 2 supports Chromecast functionality, enabling you to stream videos from your phone or compatible devices directly to the projector. This feature is particularly useful for sharing content with others or leveraging the larger projection screen for a more immersive viewing experience. By selecting the Chromecast option on your mobile device, you can easily cast the content to the MoGo 2 and enjoy it on a grander scale.

Installing Netflix on the XGIMI MoGo 2:

While the MoGo 2 offers seamless access to many popular apps, including YouTube, installing Netflix can be a bit trickier due to Netflix’s compatibility requirements on certain Android-based devices. However, there is a workaround to get Netflix up and running on the MoGo 2:

  1. Open the Google Play Store and search for “DesktopManager.”
  2. Install the DesktopManager app on the MoGo 2.
  3. Once the app is installed, grant it the necessary permissions when prompted.
  4. Open the DesktopManager app and navigate to the Netflix app.
  5. If the installation doesn’t start automatically, click on the “Install” button again.
  6. You may be prompted to allow the installation of unknown apps. Grant permission for DesktopManager to install unknown apps.
  7. Click “Install” once again, and this time, the Netflix app should start installing.
  8. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the Netflix installation process.
  9. Once installed, launch the Netflix app and sign in with your Netflix account to start enjoying your favorite shows and movies.

Gaming Experience on the XGIMI MoGo 2:

As an avid gamer, I was excited to put the XGIMI MoGo 2 to the test in the gaming department. I connected my gaming console and prepared for an immersive gaming experience.

To my delight, the MoGo 2 exceeded my expectations. The combination of its 720p resolution, vibrant colors, and HDR 10 enhanced contrast delivered visually captivating gameplay. Whether I was exploring fantastical worlds or engaging in intense battles, the MoGo 2 rendered the graphics with impressive clarity and detail.

The color representation was accurate, and the image quality remained sharp and vivid. The 400 ISO lumens ensured sufficient brightness, allowing me to fully immerse myself in the gaming experience without any distracting dimness or loss of detail. Additionally, the low input lag of the MoGo 2 contributed to a seamless and responsive gaming session.

I tested various genres of games, including action, adventure, and racing, and the MoGo 2 handled them all flawlessly. From fast-paced action sequences to breathtaking landscapes, the projector delivered smooth visuals, bringing the games to life in a larger-than-life format. The larger screen size of the projector also added to the immersion, allowing me to appreciate intricate details and immerse myself fully in the gaming world.

Power Usage and Portability:

The XGIMI MoGo 2 is designed to be a portable projector that offers flexibility in terms of power consumption. I decided to have some fun and test its power usage in different scenarios.

During my initial testing, which involved browsing, opening and closing apps on the MoGo 2, the average power consumption was around 51W. This power draw was recorded over a span of approximately 5 minutes, ensuring a representative sample.

To further gauge power usage, I conducted a 20-minute test while playing a movie with the volume set at around 75% of its maximum level. I observed that the power consumption settled around an average of 49.7W during this test. Despite the extended usage and the potential for increased power draw due to heat dissipation, the MoGo 2 efficiently maintained its power consumption, indicating optimized performance.

Finally, I tested the ECO mode, which is specifically designed for use with a portable battery bank. In this mode, the power draw dropped to an average of 42.5W and remained consistent whether I was browsing apps or watching a movie. This low power consumption demonstrates the MoGo 2’s suitability for use with a 65W or higher-capacity portable battery bank, allowing for extended outdoor usage without compromising performance.

Final Thoughts on the XGIMI MoGo 2:

The XGIMI MoGo 2 Smart Projector offers a compelling package for those seeking a portable and feature-rich projector for their audio-visual needs. Its compact design, coupled with its impressive performance, makes it a standout choice in its category.

The MoGo 2’s design embodies elegance and simplicity, while its intelligent features, such as the ISA 1.0 technology, ensure hassle-free setup and optimal viewing experiences. The self-developed audio system with Dolby support delivers immersive sound quality, enhancing the overall cinematic experience.

With Android TV 11.0 and access to Google Play, the MoGo 2 provides a vast selection of apps, eliminating the need for additional devices. The projector’s compact form factor and compatibility with a portable power bank make it a versatile option for on-the-go entertainment. Whether you’re traveling, camping, or hosting a backyard movie night, the MoGo 2 ensures that you can enjoy your favorite movies, shows, and games wherever you are.

The MoGo 2’s gaming performance is commendable, with its 720p resolution, vibrant colors, and low input lag providing an immersive gaming experience. From fast-paced action games to visually stunning adventures, the projector brings games to life on a larger screen, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in the virtual worlds.

In terms of power usage, the MoGo 2 demonstrates efficiency and adaptability. Its average power consumption of around 51W during normal usage ensures optimal performance without excessive energy consumption. Moreover, the projector’s compatibility with a 65W or higher-capacity portable power bank allows for extended usage in outdoor settings, making it an excellent choice for outdoor movie nights or camping trips.

In conclusion, the XGIMI MoGo 2 Smart Projector impresses with its compact design, user-friendly features, and exceptional performance. Whether you’re looking to create a cinematic experience at home, enjoy entertainment on the go, or indulge in immersive gaming, the MoGo 2 delivers on all fronts. With its Intelligent Screen Adaptation Technology, self-developed audio system, and seamless integration of Android TV 11.0, the MoGo 2 offers a comprehensive and hassle-free entertainment solution.

The MoGo 2’s portability, compatibility with a power bank, and extensive app library further enhance its appeal, allowing you to enjoy your favorite content anytime, anywhere. Whether you’re a movie enthusiast, a gaming aficionado, or a casual viewer, the XGIMI MoGo 2 Smart Projector is a versatile and high-performing device that offers an immersive audio-visual experience in a compact and portable package.
