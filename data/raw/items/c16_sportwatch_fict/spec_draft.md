The Veltrun R40 is a GPS running watch. The case measures 44 by 44 by 12.2 millimetres, weighs 41 grams, and fits wrists between 130 and 210 millimetres; it is offered in one 44-millimetre width. The 1.2-inch transflective colour display sits behind a mineral glass lens and stays readable in direct sunlight; at 240 by 240 pixels the resolution reads coarse close up. The silicone band, the only material offered, uses a 22-millimetre quick release fitting that swaps without tools. Five physical buttons handle every menu and the display is not a touchscreen, so the watch is operated with gloves or wet hands by pressing rather than swiping. Battery life reaches 12 days in watch mode and 24 hours in GPS mode. Positioning uses GPS and GLONASS only; there is no third satellite system. A wrist heart rate sensor, an accelerometer, and a barometric altimeter complete the sensor set, and the altimeter records elevation through every activity. History memory is 64 megabytes. Training features include an interval timer for repeat efforts, elevation profiles drawn after each recorded activity, and a recovery advisor that suggests rest days after activities. Activity profiles cover running, cycling, indoor treadmill work, and pool swimming, and each finished session is reviewed on the phone afterwards. There is no onboard music storage and no map or breadcrumb trail for navigation. The watch pairs over Bluetooth with the maker's own companion app and with no third-party app. Charging depends on a proprietary clip cable rather than a standard connector. It carries a 5 ATM rating for swimming. Included accessories: the proprietary charging clip cable and a quick start manual. Maintenance involves rinsing the case after swimming and keeping the charging contacts on the underside free of salt and grit.
