The Veltrun R40 is a GPS running watch. The case measures 44 by 44 by 12.2 millimetres, weighs 41 grams, and fits wrists between 130 and 210 millimetres. The 1.2-inch transflective colour display shows 240 by 240 pixels behind a mineral glass lens, and the silicone band uses a 22-millimetre quick release fitting. Five physical buttons handle all navigation; the display is not a touchscreen. Battery life is rated at up to 12 days in watch mode or 24 hours with GPS running. Positioning uses GPS and GLONASS, alongside a wrist heart rate sensor, an accelerometer, and a barometric altimeter that records elevation during activities. History memory is 64 megabytes. The watch pairs over Bluetooth with the maker's own companion app. It carries a 5 ATM water rating. Training features include an interval timer, elevation profiles, and a recovery advisor.
