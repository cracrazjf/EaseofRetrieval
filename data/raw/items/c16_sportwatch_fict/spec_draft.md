The Veltrun R40 is a GPS running watch. The case measures 44 by 44 by 12.2 millimetres, weighs 41 grams, and fits wrists between 130 and 210 millimetres. The 1.2-inch transflective colour display shows 240 by 240 pixels behind a mineral glass lens, a resolution that reads coarse close up, and the silicone band, the only material offered, uses a 22-millimetre quick release fitting. Five physical buttons handle all navigation and the display is not a touchscreen, so every menu is reached by pressing. Battery life is up to 12 days in watch mode or 24 hours with GPS running. Positioning uses GPS and GLONASS and no third system, alongside a wrist heart rate sensor, an accelerometer, and a barometric altimeter that records elevation during activities. History memory is 64 megabytes. There is no onboard music storage. The watch pairs over Bluetooth with the maker's own companion app and no third-party app. It carries a 5 ATM water rating. Training features include an interval timer, elevation profiles from the altimeter, and a recovery advisor. Included accessories: a charging clip cable and a quick start manual. Maintenance involves rinsing the case after swimming and keeping the charging contacts on the underside free of salt and grit.
