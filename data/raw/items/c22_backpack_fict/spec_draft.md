The Cendric Ridge 28 is a hiking daypack in a single size holding 28 litres. It weighs 1.85 pounds and measures 23.2 by 10.6 by 9.4 inches. The main compartment closes with a roll top secured by a side release buckle, so pack height varies with how much is carried. The body fabric is recycled 210 denier ripstop nylon with a water repellent finish, and the base uses recycled 400 denier packcloth. A perforated foam backpanel sits behind an adjustable torso strap that positions the harness for different back lengths. External features include daisy chain webbing, trekking pole loops, and side compression straps. Storage includes one zippered hipbelt pocket, an internal sleeve, and a front stretch pocket. The pack is designed for loads between 8 and 18 pounds. Maintenance involves brushing dirt from the backpanel, emptying the pockets, and hanging the pack to dry after wet trips.
