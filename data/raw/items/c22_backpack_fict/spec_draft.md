The Cendric Ridge 28 is a hiking daypack in a single size holding 28 litres, a single size that fits fewer torso lengths than a two-size range. It weighs 1.85 pounds, light for 28 litres, and measures 23.2 by 10.6 by 9.4 inches. The main compartment closes with a roll top secured by a side release buckle, which cinches the pack down to its contents; pack height shifts with how much is carried, the compartment is reached by unrolling the whole top, and the roll top leaves no lid pocket for small items. The body fabric is recycled 210 denier ripstop nylon with a water repellent finish, and the base uses heavier recycled 400 denier packcloth. A perforated foam backpanel, which vents less than suspended mesh, sits behind an adjustable torso strap that moves the harness to fit different back lengths. Daisy chain webbing runs down the front and takes whatever needs clipping on, trekking poles stow in loops along the side, and side compression straps pull a half-empty pack tight. There is no ice axe attachment on the exterior. Storage is one zippered hipbelt pocket rather than two, an internal sleeve sized for a hydration reservoir, and a front stretch pocket that swallows a wet layer without opening the main compartment. No rain cover is included with the pack. The pack is designed for loads of 8 to 18 pounds, and the suspension carries 18 pounds without complaint. Maintenance involves brushing dirt from the foam backpanel, rinsing the webbing after salt or grit, and hanging the pack to dry after wet trips. The buckle releases with one hand, the shoulder straps are perforated foam, and the sternum strap slides on a rail to sit where the wearer wants it. The padded hipbelt and the shoulder straps are stitched to the body rather than removable, and the harness carries the same water repellent finish as the body fabric.
