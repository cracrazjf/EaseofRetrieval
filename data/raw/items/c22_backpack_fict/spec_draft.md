The Cendric Ridge 28 is a hiking daypack in a single size holding 28 litres, which fits fewer torso lengths than a two-size range. It weighs 1.85 pounds and measures 23.2 by 10.6 by 9.4 inches. The main compartment closes with a roll top secured by a side release buckle, so pack height varies with how much is carried and the compartment is reached by unrolling the top; the roll top leaves no lid pocket for small items. The body fabric is recycled 210 denier ripstop nylon with a water repellent finish, and the base uses recycled 400 denier packcloth. A perforated foam backpanel, which vents less than a suspended mesh panel, sits behind an adjustable torso strap that positions the harness for different back lengths. External features include daisy chain webbing, trekking pole loops, and side compression straps; there is no ice axe attachment. Storage includes one hipbelt pocket rather than two, an internal sleeve, and a front stretch pocket. The pack is designed for loads between 8 and 18 pounds. Maintenance involves brushing the backpanel and hanging the pack to dry. The compression straps pull a half-empty pack tight against the back, and the stretch pocket takes a wet layer without opening the main compartment.
