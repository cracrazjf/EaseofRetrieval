The Zojirushi NS-ZCC10 is a microcomputer rice cooker and warmer for home use. It cooks up to 5.5 uncooked cups, or one liter, and measures 10 by 13-1/8 by 8-1/4 inches. The unit runs on 120-volt power at 680 watts. Menu settings cover white rice in regular, sushi, softer, and harder textures, plus mixed, porridge, sweet, semi-brown, brown, rinse-free, and quick cooking. Neuro Fuzzy logic adjusts temperature and timing during the cycle, and a spherical inner pan surrounds the rice with heat. A colored LCD panel shows the clock and a delay timer with two settings. Automatic keep warm, extended keep warm, and a reheating cycle hold or return rice to serving temperature. Included accessories: a rice measuring cup, a rinse-free measuring cup, a nonstick spatula, and a spatula holder. Maintenance involves washing the detachable inner lid and inner pan.
