The Zojirushi NS-ZCC10 is a microcomputer rice cooker and warmer for home use. It cooks up to 5.5 uncooked cups, or one litre, measures 10 by 13-1/8 by 8-1/4 inches, and runs on 120-volt power at 680 watts; the maker does not state a weight. Eleven menu settings cover white rice in regular, sushi, softer, and harder textures, plus mixed, porridge, sweet, semi-brown, brown, rinse-free, and quick cooking. There is no steam setting and no slow-cook setting among the eleven menus. Neuro Fuzzy logic adjusts temperature and timing during the cycle, and a spherical inner pan surrounds the rice with heat from every side. Owners and reviewers report consistent rice texture across grain types. The white rice cycle runs 40 to 45 minutes and the brown rice cycle 80 to 100 minutes. A colour LCD panel carries a clock and a delay timer with two settings. Automatic keep warm, extended keep warm, and a reheating cycle are selected from the same panel, and reviewers report rice holding its texture past 12 hours on keep warm. The inner lid detaches for washing, and reviewers report the removal is fiddly and needed weekly. The cord retracts into the housing, and the handles stay cool to the touch. The cooker is priced above basic rice cookers, and owner reports describe service life over a decade. It is made in Japan and cETLus listed. Included accessories: a rice measuring cup, a rinse-free measuring cup, a nonstick spatula, and a spatula holder. The two cups are sized differently, one for rice and one for rinse-free rice. Menu selection, the clock, and the delay timer share that single LCD panel. The nonstick inner pan lifts out of the housing for washing. Maintenance involves washing the detachable inner lid and the nonstick inner pan after each use, then drying both before the pan goes back into the housing.
