The Zojirushi NS-ZCC10 is a microcomputer rice cooker and warmer for home use. It cooks up to 5.5 uncooked cups, or one litre, measures 10 by 13-1/8 by 8-1/4 inches, and runs on 120-volt power at 680 watts; Zojirushi does not state a weight. Eleven menu settings cover white rice in regular, sushi, softer, and harder textures, plus mixed, porridge, sweet, semi-brown, brown, rinse-free, and quick cooking, so working through them adds steps to each meal. Neuro Fuzzy logic adjusts temperature and timing during the cycle and a spherical inner pan surrounds the rice, which owners report gives consistent rice texture across grain types. White rice takes 40 to 45 minutes and brown rice 80 to 100. A colour LCD panel shows the clock and a delay timer with two settings. Automatic keep warm, extended keep warm, and a reheating cycle hold rice at serving temperature past twelve hours. The detachable inner lid needs weekly cleaning and is fiddly to remove. The cooker is priced above basic rice cookers, and owners report service life over a decade. Included accessories: two measuring cups, a nonstick spatula, and a spatula holder. Maintenance involves washing the inner lid and the nonstick inner pan after each use.
