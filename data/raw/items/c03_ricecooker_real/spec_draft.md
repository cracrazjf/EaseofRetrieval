The Zojirushi NS-ZCC10 is a microcomputer rice cooker and warmer for home use. It cooks up to 5.5 uncooked cups, or one litre, measures 10 by 13-1/8 by 8-1/4 inches, and runs on 120-volt power at 680 watts and draws that power continuously through the cooking and keep-warm cycles. Eleven menu settings cover white rice in regular, sushi, softer, and harder textures, plus mixed, porridge, sweet, semi-brown, brown, rinse-free, and quick cooking. There is no steam setting and no slow-cook setting among the eleven menus, and no steaming basket is supplied. Neuro Fuzzy logic adjusts temperature and timing during the cycle, and a spherical inner pan surrounds the rice with heat from every side. Rice texture stays consistent across grain types. The white rice cycle runs 40 to 45 minutes and the brown rice cycle 80 to 100 minutes. A colour LCD panel carries a clock and a delay timer with two settings. The cooker has no wireless connection or app control; every setting is made on that panel. Automatic keep warm, extended keep warm, and a reheating cycle are selected from the same panel, and rice holds its texture past 12 hours on keep warm. The inner lid detaches for washing, and the removal is fiddly and needed weekly. The cord retracts into the housing, and the handles stay cool to the touch. The cooker is priced above basic rice cookers, and service life runs over a decade. It is made in Japan and cETLus listed. Included accessories: a rice measuring cup, a rinse-free measuring cup, a nonstick spatula, and a spatula holder. Maintenance involves washing the inner lid and pan after use, then drying both before the pan goes back into the housing. The cooker does not use pressure, so cycles run at the atmospheric boiling point, and the power cord is captive rather than detachable.
