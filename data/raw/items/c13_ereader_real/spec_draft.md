The Kobo Clara 2E is a six-inch e-reader for books and audiobooks. The E Ink Carta 1200 touchscreen shows 1448 by 1072 pixels at 300 pixels per inch, and text stays crisp at ordinary reading distance. A six-inch page shows less text before a turn than larger readers hold. The screen sits recessed below the bezel rather than flush, and the front light shows darker patches near the lower edge. ComfortLight PRO adjusts brightness and colour temperature together, warming the light for reading at night, and a dark mode inverts the page to white on black. The reader measures 112.05 by 159.02 by 8.66 millimetres and weighs 171 grams, light enough to hold in one hand for long stretches. There are no page-turn buttons on the bezel, so pages turn by tapping the screen. Onboard storage is 16 gigabytes, enough for up to 12,000 titles, and there is no memory card slot to add more. A 1.0-gigahertz processor drives the reader, and menus and page turns lag behind competing readers. Connections are dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth for audiobook playback to headphones, and USB-C only; there is no cellular connection. The housing is rated IPX8 for up to 60 minutes in two metres of water, and the exterior uses over 85 percent recycled plastic including ocean-bound material. Fifteen file formats open natively, OverDrive library borrowing is built in where the service is available, and articles saved to Pocket sync across to the device. Audiobooks bought from the Kobo store play to headphones or a speaker, and the 16 gigabytes of storage is shared between books, audiobook files, and the system. There is no headphone jack and no built-in speaker, so audio leaves the reader over Bluetooth. Battery life runs to weeks per charge. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves wiping the screen with a dry cloth.
