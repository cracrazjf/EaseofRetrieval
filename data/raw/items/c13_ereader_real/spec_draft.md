The Kobo Clara 2E is a six-inch e-reader for books and audiobooks. The E Ink Carta 1200 touchscreen shows 1448 by 1072 pixels at 300 pixels per inch, with a dark mode option; the screen sits recessed below the bezel rather than flush, and independent reviews report the front light showing darker patches near the lower edge. ComfortLight PRO adjusts both brightness and colour temperature for night reading. The reader measures 112.05 by 159.02 by 8.66 millimetres and weighs 171 grams, a body suited to one-handed reading, and carries no page-turn buttons. Onboard storage is 16 gigabytes, holding up to 12,000 titles, driven by a 1.0-gigahertz processor; reviewers find menus and page turns lag behind competing readers. There is no memory card slot. Connections are dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth for audiobooks, and USB-C. The housing is rated IPX8 for up to 60 minutes in two metres of water, and its exterior uses over 85 percent recycled plastic including ocean-bound material. Fifteen file formats open natively and OverDrive library borrowing is built in. Battery life is stated in weeks per charge rather than as a capacity figure. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves wiping the screen with a dry cloth.
