The Kobo Clara 2E is a six-inch e-reader for books and audiobooks. The E Ink Carta 1200 touchscreen shows 1448 by 1072 pixels at 300 pixels per inch, with a dark mode option. ComfortLight PRO adjusts both brightness and color temperature. The reader measures 112.05 by 159.02 by 8.66 millimeters and weighs 171 grams. Onboard storage is 16 gigabytes, holding up to 12,000 titles, driven by a 1.0-gigahertz processor. Connections are dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth for audiobooks, and USB-C for charging. The housing is rated IPX8 for up to 60 minutes in two metres of water, and its exterior uses over 85 percent recycled plastic including ocean-bound material. Fifteen file formats open natively, and OverDrive library borrowing is built in where the service operates. Battery life is stated in weeks per charge rather than as a capacity figure. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable and a quick start guide.
