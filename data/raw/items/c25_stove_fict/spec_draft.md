The Fyrren S1 is a remote-canister camping stove. The burner sits on the ground on three folding pot supports that stay attached, and a flexible hose connects it to a fuel canister placed alongside rather than underneath, so burner and canister together occupy a wider footprint than a top-mount stove. Including the hose the stove weighs 3.4 ounces, or 96 grams, heavier than compact top-mount stoves, and that hose adds bulk in the pack; folded it measures 3.1 by 2.4 by 1.6 inches. Output is rated at 2600 kilocalories per hour, or 3020 watts and 10,300 British thermal units, bringing two cups of water to a boil in about three minutes in still air. One eight-ounce canister runs about a hundred minutes. A needle valve on the hose fitting adjusts the flame. The burner head is stainless steel on an aluminium base, open enough to be more exposed to wind, and takes pots up to 2.5 litres. There is no igniter, so a match or lighter is carried separately. Included accessories: the hose and a stuff sack. It burns butane and isobutane mixes but not pure propane, and one eight-ounce canister runs about a hundred minutes at a moderate flame setting.
