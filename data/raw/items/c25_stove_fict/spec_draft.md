The Fyrren S1 is a remote-canister camping stove. The burner sits on the ground on three pot supports that fold flat and stay attached, and a flexible hose connects it to a fuel canister placed alongside rather than underneath, so burner and canister together take a wider footprint than a top-mount stove. The low burner keeps a full pot from tipping. Including the hose the stove weighs 3.4 ounces, or 96 grams, more than compact top-mount rivals, and the hose adds bulk a top-mount stove avoids; folded it measures 3.1 by 2.4 by 1.6 inches. Output is rated at 2600 kilocalories per hour, or 3020 watts and 10,300 British thermal units, which brings water up quickly, and two cups reach a boil in about three minutes in still air. One eight-ounce canister runs about a hundred minutes at a moderate setting. A needle valve on the hose fitting trims the flame. The hose threads onto the canister before every use. The burner head is stainless steel on an aluminium base, open enough that it catches wind from the side, and the supports take pots up to 2.5 litres, or 2 litres of liquid. Because the canister sits beside the burner it can be run inverted for liquid feed in cold weather. There is no igniter, so a match or lighter has to travel with it. It burns butane and isobutane mixes, and pure propane canisters cannot be used. Included accessories: the hose and a stuff sack. No fuel canister comes in the box. Maintenance involves keeping the burner ports clear and storing the hose without kinks. The supports fold against the burner body for stowing, the hose unscrews from the canister when the stove has cooled, and the valve sits at the canister end where it can be reached without leaning over the flame.
