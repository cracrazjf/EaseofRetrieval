The Fyrren S1 is a remote-canister camping stove. The burner sits on the ground on three folding pot supports, and a flexible hose connects it to a fuel canister placed alongside rather than underneath. Including the hose the stove weighs 3.4 ounces, or 96 grams, and folds to 3.1 by 2.4 by 1.6 inches. Output is rated at 2600 kilocalories per hour, equivalent to 3020 watts or 10,300 British thermal units, bringing two cups of water to a boil in about three minutes in still air. One eight-ounce canister runs about an hour and forty minutes. A needle valve on the hose fitting adjusts the flame. The burner head is stainless steel on an aluminium base and takes pots up to 2.5 litres. There is no built-in igniter, so a match or a separate lighter is required to start the burner. Included accessories: the hose and a stuff sack.
