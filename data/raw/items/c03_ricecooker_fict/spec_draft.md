The Hamura GR-6 is a microcomputer rice cooker and warmer for home use. It cooks up to 5.0 uncooked cups, or 0.9 litres, measures 9-1/2 by 12-3/4 by 8 inches, weighs 8.4 pounds, and runs on 120-volt power at 650 watts. Seven menu settings cover white rice in regular, softer, and harder textures, plus mixed, porridge, brown, and quick cooking; there is no sushi setting and no rinse-free setting. Microcomputer temperature control adjusts heat during the cycle and a spherical inner pan surrounds the rice. The brown rice cycle runs considerably longer than the white. A monochrome LCD panel shows the current cooking stage but no clock, and the cooker has no delay timer. Automatic keep warm and a reheating cycle hold or return rice to serving temperature. The detachable inner lid and the removable steam vent wash as separate parts. The power cord is fixed rather than retractable, and the side handles stay cool enough to grip. Included accessories: a rice measuring cup, a nonstick spatula, and a spatula holder. Maintenance involves washing the detachable lid, the removable steam vent, and the nonstick inner pan after each use, then leaving the pan to dry before it goes back into the housing.
