The Hamura GR-6 is a microcomputer rice cooker and warmer for home use. It cooks up to 5.0 uncooked cups, or 0.9 litres, measures 9-1/2 by 12-3/4 by 8 inches, weighs 8.4 pounds, and runs on 120-volt power at 650 watts. Seven menu settings cover white rice in regular, softer, and harder textures, plus mixed, porridge, brown, and quick cooking; no sushi setting or rinse-free setting appears among the seven menus. Microcomputer temperature control adjusts heat during the cycle, and a spherical inner pan surrounds the rice with heat from every side. The nonstick inner pan releases cooked rice without sticking. The brown rice cycle runs considerably longer than the white cycle. A monochrome LCD panel shows the current cooking stage and no clock or time of day. The cooker has no delay timer. One inner pan comes with the cooker, so a single batch is capped at the 5.0-cup mark. Automatic keep warm holds rice at serving temperature, and a separate reheating cycle returns cooled rice to serving temperature; there is no extended keep-warm mode beyond the automatic one. The detachable inner lid and the removable steam vent wash as separate parts. The power cord is fixed and stays out rather than winding back into the housing, and the side handles stay cool to the touch. Included accessories: a rice measuring cup, a nonstick spatula, and a spatula holder. Maintenance involves washing the detachable lid, the removable steam vent, and the nonstick inner pan after each use, then leaving the pan to dry before it goes back into the housing. Menu selection and the cooking stage share the single LCD panel. The nonstick inner pan lifts out of the housing for washing, and the measuring cup is sized to the cooker's own cup rather than a standard kitchen cup. The lid latches shut and releases with a button on the front of the housing.
