The Hamura GR-6 is a microcomputer rice cooker and warmer for home use. It cooks up to 5.0 uncooked cups, or 0.9 liters, measures 9-1/2 by 12-3/4 by 8 inches, and weighs 8.4 pounds. The unit runs on 120-volt power at 650 watts. Seven menu settings cover white rice in regular, softer, and harder textures, plus mixed, porridge, brown, and quick cooking. Microcomputer temperature control adjusts heat during the cycle, and a spherical inner pan surrounds the rice. A monochrome LCD panel shows the current cooking stage; the cooker has no delay timer. Automatic keep warm and a reheating cycle hold or return rice to serving temperature. Included accessories: a rice measuring cup, a nonstick spatula, and a spatula holder. Maintenance involves washing the detachable inner lid, the removable steam vent, and the nonstick inner pan after each use.
