The FlexiSpot E7 is a height-adjustable desk frame for home and office use. The frame travels from 22.8 to 48.4 inches, suiting users between roughly four foot two and six foot four in height. Two motors drive three-stage lifting columns at about 1.5 inches per second, and the frame is rated to carry 355 pounds of load. Construction is carbon steel, certified to BIFMA standards, and the assembled frame weighs about 76.7 pounds. An LED keypad stores four memory heights and offers a child lock and sit-stand reminders. An anti-collision function stops and reverses travel when the desk contacts an obstacle below or above it. Sound during travel stays under about 50 decibels. Included accessories: the keypad, mounting hardware, and an under-desk cable tray. Maintenance involves periodically checking the column bolts, keeping the travel path clear of obstructions, and wiping the frame with a dry cloth.
