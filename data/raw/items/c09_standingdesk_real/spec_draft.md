The FlexiSpot E7 is a height-adjustable desk frame for home and office use. The frame travels from 22.8 to 48.4 inches, suiting users between roughly four foot two and six foot four. Two motors drive three-stage lifting columns at about 1.5 inches per second, and the frame carries 355 pounds, enough for multi-monitor setups. Construction is carbon steel, certified to BIFMA standards; the assembled frame weighs about 76.7 pounds, and assembly is heavy enough to be realistically a two person job. An LED keypad stores four memory heights and offers a child lock and sit-stand reminders. An anti-collision function stops and reverses travel when the desk contacts an obstacle. Sound during travel stays under about 50 decibels, audible in a shared room. Independent testing finds the frame stable through most of the height range but with noticeable wobble at maximum height under load. Cable management amounts to one basic under-desk tray. The frame carries a fifteen year warranty on frame, motor, and electronics, and ships without a desktop. Included accessories: the keypad, mounting hardware, and the cable tray. Maintenance involves checking the column bolts periodically. The desktop is bought separately and bolts to the frame through pre-drilled mounting points along both crossbars.
