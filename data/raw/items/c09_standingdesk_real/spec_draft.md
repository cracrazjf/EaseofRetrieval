The FlexiSpot E7 is a height-adjustable desk frame for home and office use. The frame travels from 22.8 to 48.4 inches, suiting users between roughly four foot two and six foot four. Two motors drive three-stage lifting columns at about 1.5 inches per second, and the frame carries 355 pounds. Construction is carbon steel, and the frame is certified to BIFMA standards. The assembled frame weighs about 76.7 pounds, and assembly is heavy enough to be realistically a two person job. An LED keypad stores four memory heights and offers a child lock and sit-stand reminders. Control is by keypad only, with no app and no wireless connection, and a USB charging port comes only on the upgraded keypad. An anti-collision function stops and reverses travel when the desk contacts an obstacle. Sound during travel stays under about 50 decibels, audible in a shared room. Independent testing finds the frame steady through most of the height range but with noticeable wobble at maximum height under load. Cable management amounts to one basic under-desk tray. The frame carries a fifteen year warranty on frame, motor, and electronics, and ships without a desktop of any kind. The keypad mounts under the front edge of the desktop and reads out the current height in inches. The frame widens to fit desktops between 47 and 71 inches, and travel across the full range takes roughly twenty seconds at the rated speed. Included accessories: the keypad, mounting hardware, and the cable tray. Maintenance involves checking the column bolts periodically and keeping the travel path clear. The desktop is bought separately and bolts to the frame through pre-drilled mounting points along both crossbars, and the crossbar width adjusts to the top being fitted.
