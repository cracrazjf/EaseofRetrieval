The Keychron K2 is a wireless mechanical keyboard with 84 keys in a 75 percent ANSI layout. The plastic-frame version measures 313 by 123 millimetres and weighs about 663 grams; the aluminium-frame version measures 317 by 129 millimetres and weighs about 794 grams. Case height is 30 millimetres at the front and 40 at the rear with keycaps, high enough that a wrist rest is generally wanted. Gateron G Pro switches come in blue, red, and brown, and a hot-swappable variant accepts other switches without soldering. Double-shot ABS keycaps ship with both Windows and Mac legend sets, Mac function legends included; key sizes are non-standard, including a 1.75u right shift, which limits aftermarket keycap sets. ABS surfaces take on a shine where fingers land most. The backlight offers 18 patterns across four brightness levels. A 4000-milliamp-hour battery runs 240 hours with the backlight off or 72 hours with RGB, charging in about three hours. Connection is Bluetooth 5.1 to three paired devices or USB Type-C wired, polling at 1000 hertz wired and 90 wireless, with N-key rollover in wired mode only. Included accessories: a cable, a keycap puller, and the alternate keycaps.
