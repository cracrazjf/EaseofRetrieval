The Keychron K2 is a wireless mechanical keyboard with 84 keys in a 75 percent ANSI layout, keeping the arrow keys and a navigation column without a number pad. The plastic-frame version measures 313 by 123 millimetres and weighs about 663 grams; the aluminium-frame version measures 317 by 129 millimetres and weighs about 794 grams, close to 800 grams for a board carried between desks. Case height with keycaps is 30 millimetres at the front and 40 at the rear, high enough to want a wrist rest. Gateron G Pro switches come in blue, red, and brown, and a hot-swappable variant accepts other switches without soldering. Double-shot ABS keycaps ship in both Windows and Mac legend sets, and Mac function legends are uncommon on a mechanical board this size. Key sizes are non-standard, including a 1.75-unit right shift, which rules out most aftermarket keycap sets. The backlight offers 18 patterns across four brightness levels, with no finer steps between them. A 4000-milliamp-hour battery runs 240 hours with the backlight off, drops to 72 hours with RGB lighting on, and takes about three hours to charge from flat. Connection is Bluetooth 5.1 to three paired devices, and switching among them works reliably; the board also runs wired over USB Type-C. Polling is 1000 hertz wired and 90 hertz wireless. N-key rollover works in wired mode only, not over Bluetooth. Both macOS and Windows layouts are supported, and the alternate keycap set swaps the modifier legends between the two platforms. The plastic-frame and aluminium-frame versions share the same 84-key layout, switches, and battery, differing in frame material, size, and mass. Included accessories: a USB-C cable, a keycap puller, and the alternate keycap set; no wrist rest is supplied. Maintenance involves lifting keycaps periodically to clear dust from the plate.
