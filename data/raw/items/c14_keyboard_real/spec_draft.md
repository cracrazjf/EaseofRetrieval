The Keychron K2 is a wireless mechanical keyboard with 84 keys in a 75 percent ANSI layout. The plastic-frame version measures 313 by 123 millimetres and weighs about 663 grams; the aluminium-frame version measures 317 by 129 millimetres and weighs about 794 grams. Height with keycaps is 30 millimetres at the front and 40 at the rear. Gateron G Pro switches are offered in blue, red, and brown, and a hot-swappable variant accepts other switches. Double-shot ABS keycaps ship with both Windows and Mac legend sets. The backlight offers 18 patterns across four brightness levels. A 4000-milliamp-hour battery runs 240 hours with the backlight off or 72 hours with RGB, charging in about three hours. Connection is Bluetooth 5.1 to three paired devices or USB Type-C wired, with N-key rollover available in wired mode. Included accessories: a USB-C cable, a keycap puller, and the alternate keycap set. Maintenance involves lifting the keycaps periodically to clear dust.
