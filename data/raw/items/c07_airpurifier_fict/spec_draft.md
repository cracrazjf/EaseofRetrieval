The Arvelia A2 is a portable air purifier for single rooms. The rectangular tower measures 7.9 by 7.9 by 15.4 inches, weighs 8.4 pounds, and draws 52 watts from a 120-volt outlet. Rated clean air delivery is 135 cubic feet per minute, covering 960 square feet at one air change per hour or 200 square feet at 4.8 air changes, so a single room at a time is the working range. Air passes through a pre-filter, a main filter, and an activated carbon layer. The pre-filter clogs unless it is vacuumed regularly, and the main filter is a recurring purchase on a fixed schedule. A particle sensor reads the air and drives an automatic fan mode, and an indicator ring on the panel reports the current reading and stays lit the whole time the unit runs. The touch panel also selects sleep mode and four manual fan speeds. Sound output runs from 26 decibels at the lowest fan speed to 56 decibels at the highest. There is no timer, so the purifier cannot be scheduled to switch off on its own, and no app control or remote is offered. At 15.4 inches tall the tower occupies a side table or a floor corner, while the 7.9 inch base takes up less than eight inches square. The main filter lifts out of the base without tools, and the panel signals when it is spent. Included accessories: a filter installed at the factory, a user manual, and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves vacuuming the pre-filter and replacing the main filter when the panel indicates. Expected filter life is six to eight months in typical use. The tower stands against a wall or beside furniture and draws air through slots on three faces.
