The Arvelia A2 is a portable air purifier for single rooms. The rectangular tower measures 7.9 by 7.9 by 15.4 inches, weighs 8.4 pounds, and draws 52 watts from a 120-volt outlet. The rated clean air delivery is 135 cubic feet per minute, covering 960 square feet at one air change per hour or 200 square feet at 4.8 air changes, which suits a single room. Air passes through a pre-filter, a main filter, and an activated carbon layer. A particle sensor drives an automatic fan mode, and an indicator ring on the panel reports the current reading and stays lit throughout operation. The touch panel also selects sleep mode and four manual fan speeds; the unit has no timer to schedule a shutdown, and no app or remote control is offered. Sound output ranges from 26 to 56 decibels. Included accessories: a filter installed at the factory, a user manual, and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves vacuuming the pre-filter, which clogs otherwise, and replacing the main filter when the panel indicates. The tower shape sits against a wall or beside furniture, and the filter lifts out from the base without tools.
