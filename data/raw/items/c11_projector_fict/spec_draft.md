The Previk PJ4 is a portable projector for home use. It measures 168 by 124 by 112 millimetres and weighs 1.3 kilograms with the battery inside. The DLP light engine uses a 0.33-inch device to produce a 1920 by 1080 image at 350 ISO lumens, covering 85 percent of the DCI-P3 range; 350 lumens needs a darkened room. Throw ratio is 1.2 to 1, giving an image between 40 and 150 inches. Focus is set by hand on a ring on the lens barrel after each move, and keystone correction is applied digitally through the menu rather than optically. An internal battery gives about two hours of playback away from an outlet, and a 60-watt adapter powers the projector otherwise. Two 6-watt speakers handle audio. Connections include one HDMI 2.0 input, USB-C, USB-A, and a 3.5-millimetre jack, with dual-band Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.0. Android TV runs on 2 gigabytes of memory and 32 gigabytes of storage. Included accessories: the power adapter and a remote control. Maintenance involves keeping the lens and the intake vents clear of dust.
