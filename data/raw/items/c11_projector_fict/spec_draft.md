The Previk PJ4 is a portable projector for home use. It measures 168 by 124 by 112 millimetres and weighs 1.3 kilograms with the battery inside, so the battery adds real weight to carry. The DLP light engine uses a 0.33-inch digital micromirror device to produce a 1920 by 1080 image at 350 ISO lumens, covering 85 percent of the DCI-P3 range; 350 lumens needs a darkened room. Throw ratio is 1.2 to 1 and the image spans 40 to 150 inches. Focus is set by hand on a ring around the lens barrel, and the ring has to be reset after each move. Keystone correction is applied digitally through the menu rather than optically, which shrinks the image inside the frame. There is no obstacle avoidance and no automatic screen alignment. An internal battery gives about two hours of playback away from an outlet, and a 60-watt adapter powers the projector otherwise; playback continues while the adapter is connected, so the battery charges during a film. Two 6-watt speakers handle audio and thin out in a large room. Wired connections are one HDMI 2.0 input, a USB-C port, a USB-A port, and a 3.5-millimetre auxiliary jack; with only one HDMI input, two sources are swapped by cable. Wireless connections are dual-band Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.0. The Android TV platform runs on 2 gigabytes of memory and 32 gigabytes of storage. The USB-C port and the USB-A port take storage and peripherals, and the 3.5-millimetre jack feeds a wired speaker or headphones when the internal pair is not wanted. A tripod socket on the underside takes a standard mount. Included accessories: the power adapter and a remote control. Maintenance involves keeping the lens and the intake vents clear of dust, since a blocked intake raises the operating temperature.
