The Previk PJ4 is a portable projector for home use. It measures 168 by 124 by 112 millimeters and weighs 1.3 kilograms. The DLP light engine uses a 0.33-inch device to produce a 1920 by 1080 image at 350 ISO lumens, covering 85 percent of the DCI-P3 range. Throw ratio is 1.2 to 1, giving an image between 40 and 150 inches. Focus is set by a ring on the lens barrel, and keystone correction is applied digitally through the menu. An internal battery supports about two hours of playback away from an outlet, and a 60-watt adapter powers the projector otherwise. Two 6-watt speakers handle audio. Connections include USB-C, USB-A, HDMI 2.0, and a 3.5-millimeter jack, with dual-band Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5.0. Android TV runs on 2 gigabytes of memory and 32 gigabytes of storage. Included accessories: adapter and remote.
