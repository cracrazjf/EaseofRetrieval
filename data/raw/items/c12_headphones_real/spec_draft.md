The Sony WH-1000XM4 is a wireless over-ear headphone with active noise canceling. The headset weighs 254 grams and uses 40-millimeter drivers with liquid crystal polymer diaphragms. It connects over Bluetooth 5.0, supports the LDAC codec at up to 990 kilobits per second, and can hold connections to two devices at once. Frequency response is 20 hertz to 20 kilohertz on standard codecs, extending to 40 kilohertz over LDAC at a 96-kilohertz sampling rate. Sony rates battery life at up to 30 hours with noise canceling on using standard codecs, and up to 38 hours with noise canceling off; a 10-minute charge returns about five hours of playback. Wired operation runs up to 36 hours. Talk time reaches 24 hours with noise canceling active. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable, a 3.5-millimeter audio cable, a carrying case, and a plug adaptor for in-flight entertainment systems. Maintenance involves wiping the earpads.
