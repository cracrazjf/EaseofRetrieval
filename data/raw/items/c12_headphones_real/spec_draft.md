The Sony WH-1000XM4 is a wireless over-ear headphone with active noise canceling. The headset weighs 254 grams, uses 40-millimetre drivers with liquid crystal polymer diaphragms, and its consumer tuning is described as satisfying across genres. Active noise canceling draws on dual sensors per cup and is rated among the strongest in its class. Padded earcups and headband stay comfortable across long sessions. It connects over Bluetooth 5.0, supports LDAC at up to 990 kilobits per second, and holds two devices at once, though multipoint falls back to standard codecs; there is no aptX or aptX HD support and no IP water resistance rating. Frequency response is 20 hertz to 20 kilohertz, extending to 40 kilohertz over LDAC. Battery life is up to 30 hours with noise canceling on standard codecs and up to 38 hours with it off, dropping to 16 hours with LDAC and DSEE Extreme; a 10-minute charge returns about five hours. Talk time reaches 24 hours and wired operation 36. A touch gesture panel sits on the right cup and registers inconsistently, and speak-to-chat pauses playback on sounds other than speech. Included accessories: cables, a hard carrying case, and a plug adaptor.
