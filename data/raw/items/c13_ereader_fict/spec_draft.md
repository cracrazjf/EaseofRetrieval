The Lireon Q2 is a seven-inch e-reader for books and audiobooks. The E Ink touchscreen shows 1680 by 1264 pixels at 300 pixels per inch, and a dark mode inverts the page to white on black. A front light adjusts brightness and colour temperature together; brightness is set by hand, as there is no ambient light sensor for automatic adjustment. The reader measures 128 by 172 by 8.2 millimetres and weighs 195 grams in a matte polymer housing with a textured back. A seven-inch body does not fit a jacket pocket, and 195 grams is felt in the wrist over a long session. Physical page-turn buttons sit on the left bezel alongside the touchscreen. Onboard storage is 32 gigabytes driven by a 1.2-gigahertz processor, and storage is fixed with no memory card slot. Connections are dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth for audiobook playback to headphones, and USB-C for charging. The housing carries no water resistance rating, so it is not intended for use near water. Twelve file formats open natively, fewer than some readers handle, and there is no built-in library borrowing service, so borrowed titles are side-loaded over USB-C. Audiobook files are stored on the device and play over Bluetooth to headphones or a speaker, and the 32 gigabytes of storage is shared between books, audiobook files, and the system. The touchscreen turns pages as well, so the bezel buttons are one option rather than the only one. Battery life is stated in weeks per charge, with no capacity figure published. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves wiping the screen with a dry cloth and keeping the charging port free of lint. The textured back gives a grip for one hand, and the front light dims to a low level for reading in a dark room.
