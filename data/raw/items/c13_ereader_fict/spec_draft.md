The Lireon Q2 is a seven-inch e-reader for books and audiobooks. The E Ink touchscreen shows 1680 by 1264 pixels at 300 pixels per inch and offers a dark mode option. A front light adjusts both brightness and colour temperature. The reader measures 128 by 172 by 8.2 millimetres and weighs 195 grams in a matte polymer housing with a textured back; a seven-inch body does not fit a jacket pocket. Physical page-turn buttons sit on the left bezel only, alongside the touchscreen. Onboard storage is 32 gigabytes driven by a 1.2-gigahertz processor, fixed with no memory card slot. Connections are dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth for audiobooks, and USB-C for charging. The housing carries no water resistance rating, so it is not intended for use near water. Twelve file formats open natively, fewer than some readers handle. Battery life is stated in weeks per charge rather than as a capacity figure. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves wiping the screen with a dry cloth and keeping the charging port free of lint. The textured back gives a grip for one hand, and the front light dims to a low level for reading in a dark room.
