The Lireon Q2 is a seven-inch e-reader for books and audiobooks. The E Ink touchscreen shows 1680 by 1264 pixels at 300 pixels per inch and offers a dark mode option. A front light adjusts both brightness and color temperature. The reader measures 128 by 172 by 8.2 millimeters and weighs 195 grams in a matte polymer housing with a textured back. Physical page-turn buttons sit on the left bezel alongside the touchscreen. Onboard storage is 32 gigabytes, driven by a 1.2-gigahertz processor. Connections are dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth for audiobooks, and USB-C for charging. The housing carries no water resistance rating. Twelve file formats open natively. Battery life is stated in weeks per charge rather than as a capacity figure. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable and a quick start guide. Maintenance involves wiping the screen with a dry cloth and keeping the charging port free of lint.
