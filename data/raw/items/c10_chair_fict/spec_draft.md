The Tarnwell C1 is a task chair for office and home use, rated to 300 pounds. Seat height adjusts pneumatically through a lever under the seat, and the seat front curves down away from the knees. Seat depth is fixed and cannot be matched to leg length. Recline resistance is set by a manual tension knob beneath the seat pan, which needs turning again whenever a different person sits, and the back opens through about eighteen degrees. A three-position lock holds the tilt, with nothing in between. The arms are fixed in height with padded caps; they cannot be raised, widened, or pivoted. A height-adjustable lumbar pad slides up and down the back frame, though it does not move forward or back. The back is knit mesh over a moulded frame, and the seat is fabric over moulded foam. No headrest is offered for the chair. It is sold in black or grey, with arm caps to match, and rides on a five-star nylon base with 60-millimetre casters suited to hard floors and carpet. The base is nylon rather than polished aluminium, and the arm caps are moulded polyurethane over a steel arm frame. The gas cylinder is a standard size and lifts the seat through about four inches of travel. The chair carries a seven year warranty on parts. It ships in one carton at about 38 pounds, and the base, gas cylinder, and seat assemble on arrival. Included accessories: casters, the gas cylinder, and an assembly tool. Maintenance involves wiping the seat surface, vacuuming the mesh back to keep it clean, and checking the caster housings for hair and debris. Recline tension and seat height are the only settings that change once the chair is built, so fit is established at assembly.
