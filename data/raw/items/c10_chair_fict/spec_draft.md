The Tarnwell C1 is a task chair for office and home use, rated to 300 pounds. Seat height adjusts pneumatically through a lever under the seat. Seat depth is fixed and cannot be matched to leg length. Recline resistance is set by a manual tension knob beneath the seat pan, which needs turning whenever a different person sits, and a three-position lock holds the tilt with nothing in between. The arms are fixed in height with padded caps; they cannot be widened or pivoted. A height-adjustable lumbar pad slides on the back frame. The back is knit mesh over a moulded frame, and the seat is fabric over moulded foam. The chair rides on a five-star nylon base with 60-millimetre casters suited to hard floors and carpet. It carries a seven year warranty on parts. The base, gas cylinder, and seat assemble on arrival. Included accessories: casters, the gas cylinder, and an assembly tool. Maintenance involves wiping the seat surface, vacuuming the mesh back, and checking the caster housings periodically for hair and debris that can slow the wheels. Recline tension and seat height are the only settings that change once the chair is built, so fit is established at assembly.
