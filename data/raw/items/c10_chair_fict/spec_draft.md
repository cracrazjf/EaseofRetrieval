The Tarnwell C1 is a task chair for office and home use. The chair is rated to 300 pounds. Seat height adjusts pneumatically through a lever under the seat, while seat depth is fixed. Recline resistance is set by a manual tension knob beneath the seat pan, and a three-position lock holds the tilt where the sitter prefers. The arms are fixed in height with padded caps. A height-adjustable lumbar pad slides on the back frame. The back is knit mesh over a molded frame, and the seat is fabric over molded foam. The chair rides on a five-star nylon base with 60-millimeter casters. It carries a seven year warranty on parts. Included accessories: casters, the gas cylinder, and an assembly tool. Maintenance involves wiping the seat surface, vacuuming the mesh back, and checking the caster housings periodically for hair and debris that can slow the wheels.
