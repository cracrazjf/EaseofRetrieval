The Fellow Stagg EKG is a gooseneck electric kettle for pour-over brewing. It measures 282 by 172 by 196 millimetres, weighs 1400 grams including its powered base, and holds 30 ounces, or 0.9 litres, which covers one to three cups. It runs on 110- to 120-volt power at 1000 to 1200 watts, 50 to 60 hertz, and reaches brewing temperature in about three minutes at around 44 decibels. The body is 304 stainless steel with a BPA-free handle and lid pull; the steel body carries no window, so the water level is checked by opening the lid. Temperature sets to the degree from 104 to 212 degrees Fahrenheit, or 40 to 100 Celsius, though the display reads 1 to 5 degrees above the measured water at some settings. A full-colour screen and a dial carry the settings, and the screen reports the set temperature and the current temperature together. Hold mode keeps the water at temperature for 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes. A brew stopwatch times the pour, a schedule function starts heating at a set time, a pre-boil sanitisation cycle takes the water to boiling first, and an altitude setting corrects the boiling point. The gooseneck spout is shaped for directed pouring and delivers a steady, controllable stream, and it pours deliberately slowly. The cord measures 2.5 feet, and the powered base occupies its own area of counter. The kettle is priced above most electric kettles. Included accessories: the kettle body and its powered base. The lid lifts off for filling, and the handle stays cool while the element runs. The kettle lifts off the base in any orientation, and the base holds the cord underneath. Maintenance involves periodic descaling of the stainless interior, since scale builds up with use, and keeping the base contacts dry.
