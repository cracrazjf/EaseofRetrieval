The Fellow Stagg EKG is a gooseneck electric kettle for pour-over brewing. It measures 11.10 by 6.77 by 7.7 inches and weighs 1400 grams including its base. The kettle holds 30 ounces, or 0.9 liters, and runs on 110- to 120-volt power at 1000 to 1200 watts. The body is 304 stainless steel with a BPA-free handle and lid pull. Temperature is set to the degree from 104 to 212 degrees Fahrenheit. A full-color screen and dial control the schedule function, a brew stopwatch, hold mode at 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes, pre-boil sanitization, and an altitude setting. The gooseneck spout directs the stream during pouring. The power cord measures 2.5 feet. Included accessories: the kettle body and its powered base. Maintenance involves periodic descaling of the interior, wiping the stainless exterior after use, and keeping the base contacts dry and free of mineral residue.
