The Fellow Stagg EKG is a gooseneck electric kettle for pour-over brewing. It measures 282 by 172 by 196 millimetres, weighs 1400 grams including its base, and holds 30 ounces, or 0.9 litres, which covers one to three cups. It runs on 110- to 120-volt power at 1000 to 1200 watts and reaches brewing temperature in about three minutes at around 44 decibels. The body is 304 stainless steel with a BPA-free handle and lid pull. Temperature sets to the degree from 104 to 212 degrees Fahrenheit, though independent testing finds the display reading 1 to 5 degrees above the measured water at some settings. A full-colour screen and dial control a schedule function, a brew stopwatch, hold mode at 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes, pre-boil sanitisation, and an altitude setting. The gooseneck spout pours deliberately slowly to direct the stream. The cord measures 2.5 feet, and the powered base adds its own counter footprint. The kettle is priced above most electric kettles. Maintenance involves periodic descaling of the interior and keeping the base contacts dry. The lid lifts off for filling, and the handle stays cool while the element runs. Included accessories: the kettle body and its powered base.
