The COSORI Pro II is a countertop air fryer for home use. It holds a 5.8-quart, 5.5-litre square basket, measures 11.8 by 13.8 by 12.6 inches, or 30 by 35 by 32 centimetres, and weighs 12.3 pounds, which is 5.6 kilograms; that counter footprint takes a substantial block of depth. The unit runs on 120-volt household power and draws 1700 watts. Temperature is adjustable from 170 to 400 degrees Fahrenheit, equal to 77 to 204 Celsius, and the timer covers one minute to sixty minutes, with a maximum of sixty minutes and no longer setting. A touch panel selects twelve one-touch presets: steak, chicken, seafood, shrimp, frozen foods, French fries, bacon, vegetables, toast, bake, preheat, and keep warm. There is no dehydrate function among the twelve presets. Adjusted time and temperature settings can be saved in place of the factory presets. The same panel connects over WiFi to the VeSync app and to voice assistants; app control requires a WiFi network and a VeSync account. Independent measurements place fan noise around 68 decibels in operation, and reviewers time the preheat at about five minutes. Reviewers report crisp food surfaces at the upper end of the temperature range. The square basket takes a whole chicken. The unit has a single basket and no dual-zone cooking, so food sits in one layer and larger quantities cook in batches. The drawer has no viewing window, so it is pulled out to check food. The removable nonstick baskets are dishwasher safe, and the crisper plate lifts out of the basket for washing underneath. Included accessories: a 100-recipe book, a quick reference guide, and a user manual. Maintenance involves washing the basket and the crisper plate after use, by hand or in the dishwasher, and wiping the interior once it has cooled.
