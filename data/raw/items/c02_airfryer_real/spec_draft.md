The Cosori Pro II 5.8QT is an electric air fryer for countertop cooking. Its square removable basket holds 5.8 quarts (5.5 liters). The unit measures 11.8 by 13.8 by 12.6 inches, weighs 12.3 pounds, and runs on 120 volts at 1700 watts. Cooking temperature adjusts from 170 to 400 F (77 to 204 C), and the timer runs from 1 to 60 minutes. Twelve one-touch presets cover steak, chicken, seafood, shrimp, frozen foods, french fries, bacon, vegetables, toast, and baking, plus preheat and keep-warm functions; adjusted settings can be saved as custom presets. Controls sit on a front touch panel, and the VeSync app adds WiFi control with voice assistant support. Included accessories: a 100-recipe book, a quick reference guide, and a user manual. The nonstick baskets are dishwasher safe, and the exterior wipes clean with a damp cloth after cooling.
