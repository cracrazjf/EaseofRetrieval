The COSORI Pro II is a countertop air fryer for home use. It holds a 5.8-quart square basket, measures 11.8 by 13.8 by 12.6 inches, and weighs 12.3 pounds; that footprint takes a substantial block of counter depth. The unit runs on 120-volt household power at 1700 watts, a draw shared with anything else on the same circuit. Temperature is adjustable from 170 to 400 degrees Fahrenheit and the timer covers one to sixty minutes. A touch panel selects twelve presets: steak, chicken, seafood, shrimp, frozen foods, French fries, bacon, vegetables, toast, bake, preheat, and keep warm; there is no dehydrate function among them, and presets can be customised and saved. The panel also connects over WiFi to the VeSync app and to voice assistants, which requires an account. Independent measurements place fan noise near 68 decibels while running. The square basket takes a whole chicken and cooks in a single layer, so larger quantities are done in batches, and the drawer must be pulled out to check food. The basket and crisper plate carry a nonstick coating and are dishwasher safe. Included accessories: a recipe book of one hundred recipes, a quick reference guide, and a user manual.
