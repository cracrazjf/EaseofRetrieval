Duolingo is a language-learning application offering over 280 courses that cover more than 40 languages, along with separate math, music, and chess tracks; 148 of those courses were added during 2025. Lessons are short and are organised around streaks and experience points that accumulate with daily use. Every course is reachable on the free tier. That tier shows advertisements between lessons and applies a hearts system, which limits how many mistakes a learner can make before a session pauses. The paid tier removes both the advertisements and the hearts limit. Clients run on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple TV, on Android devices, and in a web browser, with progress syncing across them. Course content is delivered as exercises in reading, listening, and short written or spoken responses rather than as formal grammar instruction, and each unit ends with a review that revisits earlier material.
