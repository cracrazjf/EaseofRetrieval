The Torvik Basin 2 is a semi-freestanding three-season backpacking tent for two people. Minimum weight is 3 pounds 2 ounces and packaged weight is 3 pounds 9 ounces, packing to 17 by 6 inches. The pole assembly holds the head end upright on its own, while the foot end must be staked out before the tent takes its shape. The floor measures 82 by 48 inches, giving 27 square feet, and interior peak height is 41 inches. A single door on the left side opens onto one vestibule of 9 square feet. The rainfly is 20 denier ripstop nylon coated to 1500 millimetres with silicone, the canopy is 15 denier with mesh upper panels, and the floor is 20 denier coated to 2500 millimetres. Included accessories: an integrated footprint, stakes, guylines, and a stuff sack. Maintenance involves drying the fly and floor fully before storage.
