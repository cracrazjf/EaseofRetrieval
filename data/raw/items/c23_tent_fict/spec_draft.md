The Torvik Basin 2 is a semi-freestanding three-season backpacking tent for two people. Minimum weight is 3 pounds 2 ounces and packaged weight 3 pounds 9 ounces, packing to 17 by 6 inches. The pole assembly holds the head end upright on its own, while the foot end must be staked out before the tent takes shape, which needs ground that will hold a stake. The floor measures 82 by 48 inches, giving 27 square feet, narrow for two adults, and interior peak height is 41 inches. A single door on the left side opens onto one vestibule of 9 square feet, shared by both occupants' gear, and the inner occupant climbs over the other to get out. The rainfly is 20 denier ripstop nylon coated to 1500 millimetres with silicone, the canopy is 15 denier with mesh upper panels, light and easily torn, and the floor is 20 denier coated to 2500 millimetres. Included accessories: an integrated footprint, stakes, guylines, and a stuff sack. Maintenance involves drying the fly and floor fully before storage. The hub and pole assembly is aluminium alloy, and the mesh upper panels vent condensation out through the canopy overnight.
