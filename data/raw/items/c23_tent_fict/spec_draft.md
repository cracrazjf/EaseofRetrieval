The Torvik Basin 2 is a semi-freestanding three-season backpacking tent for two people, a three-season build that is not made for winter snow loads. Minimum weight is 3 pounds 2 ounces, light for a two-person double-wall tent, and packaged weight is 3 pounds 9 ounces, coming in under four pounds. It packs down to 17 by 6 inches. The hub and pole assembly is aluminium alloy and holds the head end upright on its own, while the foot end will not stand until it is staked, so hard ground leaves the foot end sagging. The floor measures 82 by 48 inches, giving 27 square feet, which is narrow for two adults, and interior peak height is 41 inches, enough headroom to allow sitting upright. A single door on the left side opens onto one vestibule of 9 square feet that takes both packs, but two people share a single vestibule for gear and one person climbs over the other to get out. The door has no rain gutter, so rain drips inside when the door opens in weather. The rainfly is 20 denier ripstop nylon coated to 1500 millimetres with silicone. The canopy is 15 denier ripstop nylon with mesh upper panels that vent condensation out overnight; 15 denier canopy fabric tears where it snags. The floor is 20 denier coated to 2500 millimetres. Two interior mesh pockets hold headlamps and phones. Included accessories: stakes, guylines, and a stuff sack. A footprint is built into the floor rather than sold separately, so the built-in footprint cannot be left home to save weight. Maintenance involves drying the fly and floor before storage and keeping the pole joints clean. The canopy zips shut against insects, the fly stakes out at the corners, and the guyline points sit at the two ends of the ridge. There is no gear loft and no second vestibule to add later.
