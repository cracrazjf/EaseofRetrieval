The Osprey Talon 22 is a hiking daypack. The large and extra-large size holds 22 litres and weighs 2.04 pounds; the small and medium size holds 20 litres and weighs 1.91 pounds, so the pack comes in two fixed sizes rather than one adjustable body. The larger size measures 22.05 by 9.84 by 9.06 inches. A weighed sample recorded 38.4 ounces, above the stated figure, and 2.04 pounds is heavy for 22 litres. The main fabric is bluesign approved recycled nylon in a 100 by 210 denier weave with a PFC-free water repellent finish, and the base uses heavier recycled 420 denier packcloth; the fabric and zippers hold up across seasons. An AirScape backpanel with an injection-molded framesheet holds the pack shape under load, and its ventilated channels keep air moving behind the spine. The back length adjusts to the wearer torso, and the fit is comfortable. A LidLock strap carries a helmet on the outside, Stow-on-the-Go loops let trekking poles stow without taking the pack off, an ice axe loop sits on the front, and side compression straps pull a half-empty pack tight. Nine pockets organise small items and keep them where they can be found. The two zippered hipbelt pockets are shallow and awkward to reach, and the external hydration sleeve is narrow with a non-universal hanger tab. No hydration reservoir is included with the pack. The pack is designed for loads of 10 to 20 pounds, and loads beyond 20 pounds sit outside that design range. The feature count is excessive for minimal loads. Maintenance involves emptying the pockets, brushing dirt from the backpanel, and hanging the pack to dry after wet trips. The main compartment closes with a full-length zip, and the lid pocket sits above it.
