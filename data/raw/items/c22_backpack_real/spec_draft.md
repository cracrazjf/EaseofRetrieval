The Osprey Talon 22 is a hiking daypack. The large and extra-large size holds 22 litres and weighs 2.04 pounds; the small and medium size holds 20 litres and weighs 1.91 pounds. The larger size measures 22.05 by 9.84 by 9.06 inches. The main fabric is bluesign approved recycled nylon in a 100 by 210 denier weave with a water repellent finish free of fluorinated compounds, and the base uses recycled 420 denier packcloth. An AirScape backpanel with an injection-molded framesheet and ventilated channels sits against a stretch harness and hipbelt. External attachments carry a helmet, trekking poles, and an ice axe, with side compression straps. Storage includes two zippered hipbelt pockets, an external hydration sleeve, and a front stretch mesh pocket. The pack is designed for loads between 10 and 20 pounds. Maintenance involves emptying the pockets, brushing dirt from the backpanel, and hanging the pack to dry.
