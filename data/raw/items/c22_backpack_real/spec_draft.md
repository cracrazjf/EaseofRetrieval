The Osprey Talon 22 is a hiking daypack. The large and extra-large size holds 22 litres and weighs 2.04 pounds, heavy for a daypack of that volume; the small and medium size holds 20 litres and weighs 1.91 pounds. The larger size measures 22.05 by 9.84 by 9.06 inches. The main fabric is bluesign approved recycled nylon in a 100 by 210 denier weave with a water repellent finish, and the base uses recycled 420 denier packcloth; reviewers report the fabric and zippers holding up across seasons. An AirScape backpanel with an injection-molded framesheet and ventilated channels keeps the back cooler, and an adjustable back length gives a comfortable fit. External attachments carry a helmet, trekking poles, and an ice axe, with side compression straps; reviewers describe the feature count as excessive for minimal loads. Nine pockets organise small items, including two zippered hipbelt pockets that are shallow and awkward to reach, an external hydration sleeve that is narrow with a non-universal hanger tab, and a front stretch mesh pocket exposed to abrasion. The pack is designed for loads between 10 and 20 pounds. Maintenance involves emptying the pockets, brushing dirt from the backpanel, and hanging the pack to dry after wet trips.
