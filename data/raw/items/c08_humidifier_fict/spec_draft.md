The Halvira H1 is an ultrasonic cool mist humidifier for medium rooms. It measures 10.6 by 9.1 by 13.8 inches, weighs 6.2 pounds, and draws 34 watts from a 120-volt outlet. The 1.3-gallon reservoir runs up to 30 hours on the low setting per fill and about ten hours at full output. A vibrating plate breaks water into a fine mist that leaves through a nozzle at the top of the housing. The nozzle turns to aim the mist away from nearby furniture, though mist still settles on whatever sits close to the outlet, and with hard water it deposits mineral dust on nearby surfaces. There is no fan in the housing, so the unit runs quietly. A built-in humidistat measures room humidity and cycles the output to hold a target level set on the dial, which shows both the target and the current reading; output cannot be set by hand, since the unit has no manual speeds. It does not shut off automatically when the reservoir runs dry. Water is added through a top-fill opening under a lift-off cap, so the reservoir stays fixed in the housing and does not lift out for washing at a sink. A replaceable mineral cartridge sits inside the reservoir and is bought again on schedule. The unit carries a two year limited warranty. Included accessories: one mineral cartridge and a cleaning brush. Maintenance involves wiping the reservoir and nozzle often and replacing the cartridge on schedule. The base takes up less than eleven inches across, and the cleaning brush reaches the corners of the reservoir where deposits gather. Sound during operation is rated at 32 decibels. A night light in the base can be switched off on its own, and the dial dims with it.
