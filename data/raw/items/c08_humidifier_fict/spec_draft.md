The Halvira H1 is an ultrasonic cool mist humidifier for medium rooms. It measures 10.6 by 9.1 by 13.8 inches, weighs 6.2 pounds, and draws 34 watts from a 120-volt outlet. The 1.3-gallon reservoir runs up to 30 hours on the low setting per fill. A vibrating plate breaks water into a fine mist that leaves through an adjustable nozzle at the top of the housing. A built-in humidistat measures room humidity and cycles the output to hold a target level set on the dial, which also shows the current reading. Water is added through a top-fill opening under a lift-off cap, so the reservoir stays in place during filling. A replaceable mineral cartridge sits in the reservoir. Included accessories: one mineral cartridge and a cleaning brush. Maintenance involves wiping the reservoir and nozzle after use and replacing the cartridge on schedule.
