The Halvira H1 is an ultrasonic cool mist humidifier for medium rooms. It measures 10.6 by 9.1 by 13.8 inches, weighs 6.2 pounds, and draws 34 watts from a 120-volt outlet. The 1.3-gallon reservoir runs up to 30 hours on the low setting per fill. A vibrating plate breaks water into a fine mist that leaves through an adjustable nozzle at the top of the housing; with hard water this mist deposits mineral dust, and it can settle on nearby surfaces. A built-in humidistat measures room humidity and cycles the output to hold a target level set on the dial, which also shows the current reading. Water is added through a top-fill opening under a lift-off cap, so the reservoir stays fixed in the housing and does not lift out for washing at a sink. A replaceable mineral cartridge sits inside the reservoir and is a recurring purchase. The unit carries a two year limited warranty. Included accessories: one mineral cartridge and a cleaning brush. Maintenance involves wiping the reservoir and nozzle after use and replacing the cartridge on schedule. The nozzle and reservoir need wiping often, and the cleaning brush reaches the corners of the reservoir where deposits gather.
