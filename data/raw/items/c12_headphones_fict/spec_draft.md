The Wrenfeld NX40 is a wireless over-ear headphone with active noise canceling. The headset weighs 268 grams and uses 42-millimetre dynamic drivers; that weight and its clamping force are felt on the head over a long session. Connection is Bluetooth 5.2 with the AAC and SBC codecs only, and no higher-bitrate codec is offered. The headphone pairs with one device at a time and does not support multipoint, so moving from a laptop to a phone means pairing again. Frequency response runs from 20 hertz to 20 kilohertz, and the drivers are rated from 20 hertz to 20 kilohertz for the wired connection. Rated battery life is up to 26 hours with noise canceling on and up to 34 hours with it off, and canceling can be switched off entirely to reach that longer figure. A 10-minute charge returns about four hours of playback, while a charge from empty takes about three hours. Wired playback runs up to 40 hours with the supplied cable; the USB-C port carries charging only and passes no audio. Three physical buttons on the right earcup control power, playback, and the noise canceling mode. Canceling runs at a single fixed strength, with no adjustable levels and no transparency mode. There is no companion app, so every setting is changed on the headset itself. The housing carries an IPX4 rating, which covers splashes and sweat but not immersion. The earcups fold flat against the headband for storage, the headband adjusts through a sliding arm on each side, and the earpads are replaceable when the covering wears. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable, a 3.5-millimetre audio cable, and a fabric carrying pouch rather than a hard case. Maintenance involves wiping the earpads and keeping the charging port clear of lint, and the pouch holds the folded headset and both cables together.
