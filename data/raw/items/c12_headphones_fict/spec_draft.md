The Wrenfeld NX40 is a wireless over-ear headphone with active noise canceling. The headset weighs 268 grams and uses 42-millimetre dynamic drivers; that clamping weight is felt over long sessions. It connects over Bluetooth 5.2 with the AAC and SBC codecs only, with no higher bitrate codec offered, and pairs with a single device at a time rather than supporting multipoint. Frequency response runs from 20 hertz to 20 kilohertz. Rated battery life is up to 26 hours with noise canceling on and up to 34 hours with it off; a 10-minute charge returns about four hours, and wired operation runs up to 40 hours with the supplied cable. Three physical buttons on the right earcup control power, playback, and the noise canceling mode; they take practice to distinguish blind. The housing carries an IPX4 rating, which covers splashes but not immersion. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable, a 3.5-millimetre audio cable, and a fabric carrying pouch rather than a hard case. Maintenance involves wiping the earpads and keeping the charging port clear of lint. The earcups fold flat against the headband for storage, and the noise canceling mode can be switched off entirely to extend playback.
