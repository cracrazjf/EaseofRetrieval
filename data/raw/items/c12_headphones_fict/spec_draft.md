The Wrenfeld NX40 is a wireless over-ear headphone with active noise canceling. The headset weighs 268 grams and uses 42-millimeter dynamic drivers. It connects over Bluetooth 5.2 with the AAC and SBC codecs and pairs with one device at a time. Frequency response runs from 20 hertz to 20 kilohertz. Rated battery life is up to 26 hours with noise canceling on and up to 34 hours with it off; a 10-minute charge returns about four hours of playback. Wired operation runs up to 40 hours with the supplied cable. Three physical buttons on the right earcup control power, playback, and the noise canceling mode. The housing carries an IPX4 rating for splash resistance. Included accessories: a USB-C charging cable, a 3.5-millimeter audio cable, and a fabric carrying pouch. Maintenance involves wiping the earpads and keeping the charging port clear.
