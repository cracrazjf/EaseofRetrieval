Review | Belkin BoostCharge Power Bank 10K 

Julian Price27 February 2024, 12:08 PM15 August 2025, 11:05 PM

Belkin’s BoostCharge PowerBank 10K sets a new standard for portable charging solutions. Its sleek design exudes premium quality while maintaining a compact form factor that easily slips into any pocket, surpassing the size of most modern smartphones.

During my testing, the BoostCharge proved its worth. My iPhone 15 Plus remained fully charged throughout a lengthy domestic flight and continued to provide power over the subsequent day. It even extended my gaming session on the PlayStation Portal. 

READ MORE: Review | Hands-on with the PlayStation Portal ‘A Taste of the Future’

Equipped with two USB-A ports and a USB-C port, the BoostCharge allows for simultaneous charging of multiple devices. Using USB-A, I achieved a commendable 10% charge on my iPhone 15 Plus in just 15 minutes. You also have the option of using the USB-C port for faster-charging thanks to its higher voltage.

Convenient battery level indication is provided by a small button and four LED lights, offering insight into the remaining charge. 

READ MORE: The Ember Mug 2 is the ‘Secret weapon in the art of perfectly heated sips’

Belkin says its BoostCharge Power Bank 10K boasts an impressive 40 extra hours of battery life for your iPhone (or any device) with this power bank — while I haven’t taken it that far, it could easily go that far. 

Environmental consciousness is also at the forefront with the BoostCharge, featuring a construction comprising a minimum of 72% post-consumer recycled content. 

What’s in the box?

The unboxing experience is delightfully sustainable, with 100% plastic-free packaging.

Included in the package is a USB-A to USB-C cable, ensuring compatibility with various devices. The power bank is available in black, blue, and pink, catering to diverse preferences.

Priced competitively at $59.95, the BoostCharge Power Bank 10K is accessible through various retailers, including Belkin’s official channels and stores such as JB HiFi, Harvey Norman, Officerworks and Amazon.

Belkin’s also just released a Hybrid Wall Charger/Battery pack, ideal for travelers and emergency situations alike. 

READ MORE: Review | Nitro Deck for Nintendo Switch

I’m on the road a lot for work and always need to stay online, often carrying multiple power banks in my bag. Another product worth a shoutout is the Magnetic Wireless Power Bank 5K, half the capacity of the BoostCharge, but its size makes up for it.

The Belkin BoostCharge Power Bank 10K was supplied to Quest Daily for the purpose of this review.
