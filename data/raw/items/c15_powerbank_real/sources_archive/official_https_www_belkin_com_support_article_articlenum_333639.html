Getting to know the Belkin BOOST↑CHARGE™ Power Bank 10K, BPB011

The Belkin BOOST↑CHARGE™ Power Bank 10K, BPB011 is a powerful portable power bank that provides up to 40 hours of extra battery life.  This 10,000 mAh power bank can charge three devices at once with a power output of 15W using its USB-C® and USB-A ports.  For more information, click here.



Product Features
 

  • 10,000 mAh capacity
  • One USB-C port
  • Two USB-A ports
  • Includes a 6-inch USB-C to USB-A cable
  • Four indicator lights
  • 2-year limited warranty
  • $2,500 Connected Equipment Warranty (CEW)


Power Specifications
 

Input: 

 

  • USB-C ⎓ 5V/3A (15W max)      
 
Output: 

 

  • USB-C ⎓ 5V/3A (15W max)
  • USB-A #1 ⎓ 5V/2.4A (15W max)
  • USB A #2 ⎓ 5V/2.4A (15W max)
