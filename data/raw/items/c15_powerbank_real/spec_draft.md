The Belkin BoostCharge Power Bank 10K is a portable battery for phones and small devices. Capacity is 10,000 milliamp-hours. One USB-C port handles both input and output, and two USB-A ports provide further outputs, so three devices charge from the pack at the same time. The USB-C port delivers 5 volts at 3 amps and each USB-A port 5 volts at 2.4 amps, with 15 watts available in total across the three ports, so three devices sharing the pack split that figure between them. No fast charge protocol is supported, neither USB Power Delivery nor Quick Charge, so the 15-watt ceiling applies to every device connected. A phone that supports fast charging draws the same 15 watts as any other device. The two USB-A ports send power out only and cannot carry a charge into the pack; the pack itself refills through the USB-C port at 5 volts and 3 amps. The pack accepts input on the same USB-C port it charges from, so refilling it occupies the port that would otherwise power a device. Four indicator lights report the remaining charge in quarters, and there is no numeric display. Every device connects to the pack by cable; there is no wireless charging pad on the case. A full pack adds up to 40 hours of battery life. The pack is compact for a 10,000 milliamp-hour battery, and the housing is solid rather than hollow. The three ports charge a phone, a watch, and earbuds together, a combination that suits several small devices at once. Included accessories: a six-inch USB-A to USB-C cable, which is short for charging a phone in a bag; no wall charger is supplied in the box. The pack carries a two-year limited warranty together with connected equipment coverage for devices damaged while charging. Maintenance involves keeping the ports free of lint and recharging the pack periodically while it is stored.
