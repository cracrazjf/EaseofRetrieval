The Belkin BoostCharge Power Bank 10K is a portable battery for phones and small devices. Capacity is 10,000 milliamp-hours. One USB-C port handles both input and output, and two USB-A ports provide further outputs, so three devices charge from the pack at the same time. The USB-C port delivers 5 volts at 3 amps and each USB-A port 5 volts at 2.4 amps, with 15 watts available in total across the three ports, so three devices sharing the pack split that figure between them. No fast charge protocol is supported, neither USB Power Delivery nor Quick Charge, so the 15-watt ceiling applies to every device connected. A phone that supports fast charging therefore draws the same 15 watts from this pack as any other device, and topping up a large phone takes correspondingly longer. The two USB-A ports send power out only; the pack itself refills through the USB-C port at 5 volts and 3 amps. Belkin does not publish a recharge time for the pack. Four indicator lights report the remaining charge in quarters, and there is no numeric display. Belkin states that a full pack adds up to 40 hours of battery life. Reviewers describe the pack as compact for a 10,000 milliamp-hour battery and the housing as solid rather than hollow. Reviewers also note the three ports charging a phone, a watch, and earbuds together. Included accessories: a six-inch USB-A to USB-C cable, which is short for charging a phone in a bag; no wall charger is supplied. The pack carries a two-year limited warranty together with connected equipment coverage for devices damaged while charging. Maintenance involves keeping the ports free of lint and recharging the pack periodically while it is stored.
