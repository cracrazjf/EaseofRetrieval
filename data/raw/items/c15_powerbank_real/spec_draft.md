The Belkin BoostCharge Power Bank 10K is a portable battery for phones and small devices. Capacity is 10,000 milliamp-hours. One USB-C port handles both input and output, and two USB-A ports provide additional outputs, so up to three devices can charge at once. The USB-C port delivers 5 volts at 3 amps, and each USB-A port delivers 5 volts at 2.4 amps, with 15 watts available in total across the ports. Input is over the same USB-C port at 5 volts and 3 amps. Four indicator lights on the housing report the remaining charge. Belkin states that a full pack adds up to 40 hours of battery life. Included accessories: a six-inch USB-A to USB-C cable. The pack carries a two-year limited warranty together with connected equipment coverage. Maintenance involves keeping the ports free of lint and recharging the pack periodically when it is stored unused for long periods.
