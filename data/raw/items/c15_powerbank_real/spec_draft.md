The Belkin BoostCharge Power Bank 10K is a portable battery for phones and small devices. Capacity is 10,000 milliamp-hours. One USB-C port handles both input and output and two USB-A ports provide additional outputs, so up to three devices charge at once. The USB-C port delivers 5 volts at 3 amps and each USB-A port 5 volts at 2.4 amps, with 15 watts available in total across the ports; there is no fast charge protocol support, so that 15-watt ceiling applies to every device. Input is over the same USB-C port at 5 volts and 3 amps, and Belkin does not specify a recharge time. Four indicator lights report the remaining charge in coarse steps. Belkin states that a full pack adds up to 40 hours of battery life. Dimensions and weight are not confirmed on Belkin's own pages, and retailer figures conflict. Independent reviews describe the housing as solid. Included accessories: a six-inch USB-A to USB-C cable. The pack carries a two-year limited warranty together with connected equipment coverage. Maintenance involves keeping the ports free of lint and recharging the pack periodically during storage. The three ports operate independently, so a phone, a watch, and a set of earbuds draw from the pack at the same time.
