The Steelcase Series 1 is a task chair for office and home use, rated to 400 pounds. Seat height adjusts pneumatically, with two height ranges offered to suit different users, and seat depth adjusts across a 2-1/4 inch span. A weight-activated synchro mechanism sets recline resistance from the sitter's own weight, with an added tension range and a seat-back angle from 100 to 122 degrees. The arms adjust in four directions: height, width, depth, and pivot; they do not lock and shift under pressure. Lumbar support is adjustable and fitted as standard, though some testers report the lumbar support and the backrest height insufficient, and half of testers found comfort falling off past four or five hours. The back is offered in 3D Microknit mesh, which stays breathable in warm rooms, or in polypropylene, and the seat in fabric, leather, or vinyl. A headrest is a separate purchase. The chair arrives assembled and usable without tools, and its adjustment range is unusual at this price point. It carries a limited lifetime warranty with twelve years of multi-shift parts and labour coverage. Maintenance involves wiping the seat and clearing the caster housings.
