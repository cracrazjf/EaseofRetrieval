The Steelcase Series 1 is a task chair for office and home use, rated to 400 pounds. It offers nine adjustment points across the seat, back, and arms. Seat height adjusts pneumatically, with two height ranges offered and chosen at order, and seat depth adjusts across a 2-1/4 inch span. A weight-activated synchro mechanism sets recline resistance from the sitter's own weight, with an added tension range, and the back opens from 100 to 122 degrees. The arms adjust in four directions, height, width, depth, and pivot; they do not lock and shift under pressure. Lumbar support is adjustable and fitted as standard, though the lumbar pad is too soft and the backrest ends too low for taller sitters. Comfort falls off past four or five hours. The back is offered in 3D Microknit mesh, which keeps air moving in a warm room, or in moulded polypropylene, and the seat in fabric, leather, or vinyl. A headrest is a separate purchase and is not fitted as standard. The seat pan is foam over a moulded shell, and its front edge curves down away from the knees. Casters are specified for carpet or for hard floor at order. The chair arrives assembled and is usable without tools. It carries a limited lifetime warranty with twelve years of multi-shift parts and labour coverage. Upholstery colours are specified at order, and the chair ships in one carton and rolls out ready to sit on. Carbon footprint is lower and recycled content higher than in the previous generation. Maintenance involves wiping the seat and clearing hair from the caster housings, which gather it over time. The arm caps are moulded polyurethane, and the chair stands on a five-star base rolling on casters.
