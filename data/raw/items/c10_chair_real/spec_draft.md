The Steelcase Series 1 is a task chair for office and home use. The chair is rated to 400 pounds. Seat height adjusts pneumatically, and Steelcase offers two height ranges to suit different users. Seat depth adjusts across a 2-1/4 inch span. A weight-activated synchro mechanism sets recline resistance from the sitter's own weight, with an added tension range and a seat-back angle from 100 to 122 degrees. The arms adjust in four directions: height, width, depth, and pivot. Lumbar support is adjustable and fitted as standard. The back is offered in 3D Microknit mesh or polypropylene, and the seat in fabric, leather, or vinyl. The chair carries a limited lifetime warranty with twelve years of multi-shift parts and labor coverage. Included accessories: the assembled chair with casters fitted at the factory. Maintenance involves wiping the seat surface, vacuuming the mesh back, and checking the caster housings periodically for debris.
