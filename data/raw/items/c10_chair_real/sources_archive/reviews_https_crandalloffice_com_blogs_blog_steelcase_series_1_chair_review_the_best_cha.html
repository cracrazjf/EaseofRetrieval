Steelcase Series 1 Chair Review: The Best Chair Under $500

Ryan Snyder | August 21, 2024

Steelcase Series 1 Chair Review: The Best Chair Under $500

Hi there, Dave with Crandall Office, and today we’re going to be taking a closer look at what we believe to be the absolute best chair you can buy for under $500, the Steelcase Series 1 Chair. So without further ado, let’s get started.

Technical Specifications

We’re not going to bore you with reading off all the technical specs, but all that information will be in the description below. A couple of things worth noting:

•Warranty: The Steelcase Series 1 comes with a 12-year warranty.

•Weight Capacity: Rated at 400 pounds.

•Assembly: Assembled in Grand Rapids, Michigan.

For more specific measurement information, be sure to check the details below.

Sizing and Fit

The Series 1 is designed to fit a broad range of people. It’s a smaller-framed chair with a shorter back, making it ideal for individuals of average build and height. However, if you’re taller or larger, this might not be the best option for you.

For those over six feet, Steelcase offers an optional headrest, increasing the back height and providing better upper back support.

Customization Options

The Steelcase Series 1 offers a variety of customization options:

•Colors: Choose from a wide array of back and seat colors.

•Frame Colors: Black or Seagull (White).

•Arm Options: Armless, height-adjustable, or 4D arms.

•Casters: Soft floor (carpet) or hard floor casters.

•Additional Options: Stool and headrest options are also available.

Steelcase did a great job of ensuring that you can tailor this chair to your specific needs.

Ergonomics and Adjustability

Personally, after testing the Series 1 for over a week, I found that it encourages a very upright and ergonomic sitting position. Some of the key adjustments include:

•Seat Depth and Height Adjustment: Customize the seat to your preferred depth and height.

•Tilt Lock: Lock the back in an upright position.

•Lean Back Pressure Settings: Two settings for lean-back pressure, regulated by your weight.

•Adjustable Lumbar: Standard on the Series 1, with an optional headrest.

For a more detailed explanation on how to adjust the chair, check out the video linked below.

Comfort

Comfort is subjective, but here’s what our staff thought:

•Seat Comfort: The seat is quite firm. Initially, some found it uncomfortable, but after extended use, most people found it more comfortable as their bodies adjusted.

•Back Comfort: Mixed reviews. The back height is shorter than other chairs, which some found limiting, but the breathable mesh back was a hit, especially in warmer climates.

•Arm Comfort: The 4D arms are highly adjustable, but the pads are smaller and firmer than other designs. Despite this, they were generally well-received.

Aesthetic Appeal

The Series 1 boasts a sleek, modern design that’s compact and minimalistic. With numerous color options and customization possibilities, it’s easy to match it to any workspace.

Durability and Build Quality

Despite its compact design and plastic components, the Series 1 is built to last. Steelcase has a solid track record of producing durable chairs, and the Series 1 is no exception. The mechanism, while utilizing both plastic and metal parts, is robust and well-built. Though the arms may feel less sturdy, they’ve held up well over the four years this chair has been in production.

Pricing and Policies

As of May 2022, you can purchase a new Steelcase Series 1 chair with 4D arms for $487. It’s a competitive price point, especially when considering the 12-year warranty and free return policy. Steelcase has managed to enter the mid-to-lower price market without compromising on quality or service.

If you’re looking to save even more, check out Crandall Office for authorized factory return chairs at a discounted price.

Conclusion

In conclusion, the Steelcase Series 1 is the best chair for under $500. It offers great build quality, comfort, ergonomics, and an impressive return policy. It’s a mid-market chair that fits most people, and we’re confident you won’t be disappointed with this purchase.
