The Tavro Flask is a vacuum-insulated beverage bottle. The 20-ounce size holds 590 millilitres, measures 10.2 by 3.2 by 3.2 inches, and weighs 1.0 pound empty before anything goes in. The body is 18/8 stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation, and the cap and cup components are BPA-free. Stated retention is 16 hours for hot contents and 22 hours for cold. A screw-off cap seals against a gasket when closed and comes off entirely before pouring, so there is no pour-through stopper; the wide opening left behind takes ice cubes and a cleaning brush. The gasket is pulled out and dried separately during cleaning, and a spare cap gasket is supplied in the box. The powder-coated exterior chips where it rubs inside a pack, and the 3.2-inch body is wider than a standard vehicle cup holder, so it does not sit in one. A fixed loop is moulded into the collar for clipping to a pack, and that loop offers no full-hand grip. The outer lid doubles as a serving cup. At 10.2 inches the bottle stands tall in a pack. The bottle is offered in several exterior colours, and the same cap and cup fit the other sizes in the line. Included accessories: the serving cup lid and a spare cap gasket. The cap threads onto the collar with a half turn, and the serving cup holds roughly a third of what the bottle carries. The base is flat and the bottle stands upright on a table without a stand. Maintenance involves hand washing the body with warm water instead of using a dishwasher, taking the cap apart to clean the gasket seat, and drying all of the parts separately before they are reassembled for storage between uses.
