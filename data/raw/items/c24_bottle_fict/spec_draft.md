The Tavro Flask is a vacuum-insulated beverage bottle. The 20-ounce size holds 590 millilitres, measures 10.2 by 3.2 by 3.2 inches, and weighs 1.0 pound empty before anything goes in. The body is 18/8 stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation and BPA-free components. Stated retention is 16 hours for hot contents and 22 hours for cold. A screw-off cap seals against a gasket when closed and is removed entirely before pouring; that gasket is removed and dried separately during cleaning. The powder-coated exterior, which chips where it rubs inside a pack, carries a fixed loop for clipping or carrying, a loop that offers no full-hand grip. The outer lid doubles as a serving cup. At 10.2 inches the bottle stands tall in a pack. Included accessories: the serving cup lid and a spare cap gasket. Maintenance involves hand washing the body with warm water rather than using a dishwasher, disassembling the cap to clean the gasket seat, and drying all of the parts separately before they are reassembled for storage between uses. The bottle is offered in several exterior colours, and the same cap and cup design carries across the sizes in the line.
