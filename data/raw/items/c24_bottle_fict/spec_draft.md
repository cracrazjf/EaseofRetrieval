The Tavro Flask is a vacuum-insulated beverage bottle. The 20-ounce size holds 590 milliliters, measures 10.2 by 3.2 by 3.2 inches, and weighs 1.0 pound empty. The body is 18/8 stainless steel with double-wall vacuum insulation and BPA-free components. Stated retention is 16 hours for hot contents and 22 hours for cold. A screw-off cap seals against a gasket when closed and is removed fully for pouring. The powder-coated exterior carries a fixed loop for clipping or carrying, and the outer lid doubles as a serving cup. The bottle is offered in several exterior colors, and the cap and cup design carries across the sizes in the line. Included accessories: the serving cup lid and a spare cap gasket. Maintenance involves hand washing the body with warm water, disassembling the cap to clean the gasket seat, and drying all of the parts separately before they are reassembled for storage between uses.
