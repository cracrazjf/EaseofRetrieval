The Kelvane Slate 65 is a wired mechanical keyboard with 68 keys in a 65 percent ANSI layout. The aluminium frame measures 315 by 105 millimetres and weighs about 810 grams, standing 32 millimetres at the front and 42 at the rear with keycaps fitted. The plate is gasket mounted over foam damping. Hot-swappable sockets accept linear or tactile switches without soldering. Dye-sublimated PBT keycaps carry legends that do not wear off with use. A white backlight offers eight patterns across four brightness levels. The board connects by USB Type-C only, polling at 1000 hertz, with full N-key rollover. Both macOS and Windows layouts are supported through a switch on the underside. Included accessories: a USB-C cable, a keycap puller, and a switch puller. Maintenance involves lifting the keycaps periodically to clear dust from the plate and wiping the aluminium frame with a dry cloth after handling.
