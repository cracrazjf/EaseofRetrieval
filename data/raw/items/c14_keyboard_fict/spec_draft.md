The Kelvane Slate 65 is a wired mechanical keyboard with 68 keys in a 65 percent ANSI layout. There is no dedicated function row and no separate navigation cluster: the F-keys, home, end, page up and page down are all reached through a layer combination. The aluminium frame measures 315 by 105 millimetres and weighs about 810 grams, a mass that keeps the board planted on a desk but makes it heavy to carry anywhere. Case height with keycaps fitted is 32 millimetres at the front and 42 at the rear, and no wrist rest is supplied. The plate is gasket mounted over foam damping, which softens the sound of each press. Hot-swappable sockets accept linear or tactile switches without soldering; switches are bought separately and are not included with the board. Dye-sublimated PBT keycaps carry legends that do not wear off with use. A white backlight offers eight patterns across four brightness levels and no other colour. The board connects by USB Type-C only, with no Bluetooth and no wireless receiver; the cable detaches at the board for storage. Polling runs at 1000 hertz with full N-key rollover across every key. The 68-key layout keeps a dedicated arrow cluster and a delete key in the right-hand column. Foam under the plate damps the case as well, so the board sounds lower than an unfilled one. Both macOS and Windows layouts are supported through a switch on the underside, which changes the modifier order without any configuration software; no software is offered, so keys cannot be remapped beyond that switch. Included accessories: a detachable USB-C cable, a keycap puller, and a switch puller. Maintenance involves lifting the keycaps periodically to clear dust from the plate, and the same board moves between machines without setup.
