The Kelvane Slate 65 is a wired mechanical keyboard with 68 keys in a 65 percent ANSI layout. There is no dedicated function row; those keys are reached through a layer combination. The aluminium frame measures 315 by 105 millimetres and weighs about 810 grams, standing 32 millimetres at the front and 42 at the rear with keycaps fitted, so it is heavy to carry anywhere. The plate is gasket mounted over foam damping. Hot-swappable sockets accept linear or tactile switches without soldering; switches are bought separately from the board. Dye-sublimated PBT keycaps carry legends that do not wear off with use. A white backlight offers eight patterns across four brightness levels and no other colour. The board connects by USB Type-C only, with no Bluetooth and no wireless receiver, so a cable tethers it to the machine; polling runs at 1000 hertz with full N-key rollover. Both macOS and Windows layouts are supported through a switch on the underside. Included accessories: a USB-C cable, a keycap puller, and a switch puller. Maintenance involves lifting the keycaps periodically to clear dust from the plate. The underside switch changes the modifier order without any software, so the same board moves between machines.
