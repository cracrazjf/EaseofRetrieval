The De'Longhi Dedica EC685 is a pump-driven espresso machine for home use. It measures 14.9 cm in width, 33 cm in depth, and 30.5 cm in height, and weighs 4.2 kg. The machine uses a 15-bar pump and a thermoblock heating system. The removable water tank holds 1.1 liters and includes a level indicator. Three illuminated buttons control single espresso, double espresso, and steam; shot volume and brewing temperature are programmable across three levels. A manual frother switches between steamed milk and froth, and also dispenses hot water. The filter holder accepts ground coffee for one or two cups as well as ESE paper pods. A removable two-level drip tray accommodates cups up to 12 cm tall, and a cup rest sits on top. The machine shuts off automatically 20 minutes after the last brew. Included accessories: a combined measuring scoop and tamper.
