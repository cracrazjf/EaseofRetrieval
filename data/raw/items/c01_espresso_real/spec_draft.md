The De'Longhi Dedica EC685 is a pump-driven espresso machine for home use. It measures 149 by 330 by 305 millimetres and weighs 4.22 kilograms. The machine uses a 15-bar pump and a thermoblock heating system; independent reviews time the warm-up at 30 to 40 seconds. The single thermoblock serves both brewing and steaming, so the two cannot run at the same time. The removable water tank holds 1.1 litres and carries a level indicator. Three illuminated buttons control single espresso, double espresso, and steam; shot volume and brewing temperature are programmable across three levels. A manual Panarello frothing sleeve switches between steamed milk and froth and also dispenses hot water; reviewers report that fine microfoam takes practice with this stock wand. The filter holder takes ground coffee for one or two cups as well as ESE paper pods, using pressurised baskets that reviewers describe as limiting espresso clarity. A removable two-level drip tray takes cups up to 12 centimetres tall, and a cup rest sits on top. The machine shuts off 20 minutes after the last brew. Measured brew temperature runs near 65 degrees Celsius unless holder and cup are preheated, and the light body shifts as the portafilter is seated. Included accessories: a combined measuring scoop and tamper; no milk pitcher is supplied.
