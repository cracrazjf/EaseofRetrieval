The Vestrino M2 is a pump-driven espresso machine for home use. It measures 145 by 320 by 310 millimetres and weighs 4.0 kilograms. The machine uses a 15-bar pump and a thermoblock heating system. Because a single thermoblock serves both functions, brewing and steaming cannot run at the same time. The removable water tank holds 1.0 litres and carries a level indicator. Two illuminated buttons control single and double espresso, with a separate dial for steam; shot volume is programmable across two levels. A manual frother switches between steamed milk and froth and also dispenses hot water, and reaching fine microfoam depends on technique. The filter holder takes ground coffee for one or two cups using two pressurised baskets; it does not accept ESE pods, and the machine has no grinder. A removable two-level drip tray takes cups up to 11 centimetres tall. There is no cup rest on top of the housing and no pressure gauge on the panel. The machine drops into standby 15 minutes after the last brew. Included accessories: a combined measuring scoop and tamper moulded as one plastic piece. Maintenance involves periodic descaling, signalled by a panel light, and rinsing the removable tank and tray.
