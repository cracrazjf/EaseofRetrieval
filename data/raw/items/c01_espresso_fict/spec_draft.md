The Vestrino M2 is a pump-driven espresso machine for home use. It measures 14.5 cm in width, 32 cm in depth, and 31 cm in height, and weighs 4.0 kg. The machine uses a 15-bar pump and a thermoblock heating system. The removable water tank holds 1.0 liters and includes a level indicator. Two illuminated buttons control single and double espresso, with a separate dial for steam; shot volume is programmable across two levels. A manual frother switches between steamed milk and froth, and also dispenses hot water. The filter holder accepts ground coffee for one or two cups, using two included pressurized baskets. A removable two-level drip tray accommodates cups up to 11 cm tall. The machine switches to standby 15 minutes after the last brew. Included accessories: a combined measuring scoop and tamper. Maintenance involves periodic descaling, indicated by a panel light, and rinsing the removable tank and tray.
