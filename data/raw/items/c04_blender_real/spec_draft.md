The Vitamix E310 Explorian is a countertop blender for home use. It measures 9 by 8 by 18 inches assembled and weighs 10.5 pounds, a mass that keeps the base planted while the motor runs. A two-horsepower motor runs on 110- to 120-volt power and drives laser-cut stainless steel blades through a metal drive system. Reviewers report smooth results on frozen fruit and nut butter. The 48-ounce container is dishwasher safe, and its height fits under standard kitchen cabinets; reviewers put its usable volume nearer 32 to 36 ounces than the 48 on the label. The blade assembly is fixed in the container and does not come out for washing. A variable speed dial offers ten settings and moves gradually rather than in steps, and a separate switch engages pulse; the speed dial carries no preset programs. Independent measurements place sound output between 88 and 104 decibels above speed five. Thick blends need tamping with the supplied mini-tamper to keep ingredients moving toward the blades. The power cord measures 4.5 feet, which fixes how far the base can sit from an outlet. Included accessories: the motor base, the 48-ounce container, and the mini-tamper; one container size is supplied and no smaller jar comes with it. Reviewers place the machine at the entry point of the manufacturer's own range. The unit carries a five-year full warranty covering parts, performance, labour, and shipping. Maintenance involves running the container with dish soap and warm water for 30 to 60 seconds, a self-clean cycle that avoids disassembly, then rinsing the container and leaving it to air dry before the next use. The container, the lid, and the tamper are the only parts that come apart for washing, and none of them need tools. The dial, the pulse switch, and the on-off switch are the whole of the control panel.
