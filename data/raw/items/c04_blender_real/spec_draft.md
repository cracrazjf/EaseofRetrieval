The Vitamix E310 Explorian is a countertop blender for home use. It measures 9 by 8 by 18 inches assembled and weighs 10.5 pounds. A two-horsepower motor runs on 110- to 120-volt power and drives laser-cut stainless steel blades through a metal drive system, handling frozen fruit and nut butter to a smooth result. The 48-ounce container is dishwasher safe; its usable volume is nearer 32 to 36 ounces in practice, and its height fits under kitchen cabinets. A variable speed dial offers ten settings and a separate switch engages pulse; the dial carries no preset programs. Independent measurements place sound output between 88 and 104 decibels above speed five. Thick blends need tamping with the supplied mini-tamper to keep ingredients moving toward the blades. The power cord measures 4.5 feet. Included accessories: the motor base, the 48-ounce container, and the mini-tamper; no smaller container is supplied. The unit carries a five-year full warranty covering parts, performance, labour, and shipping. Maintenance involves running the container with dish soap and warm water for 30 to 60 seconds, a self-clean cycle that avoids disassembly, then rinsing and air drying. The container, lid, and tamper are the only parts that come apart for washing, and none of them need tools.
