The Vitamix E310 Explorian is a countertop blender for home use. It measures 9 by 8 by 18 inches and weighs 10.5 pounds. A two-horsepower motor runs on 110- to 120-volt household power and drives laser-cut stainless steel blades through a metal drive system. The 48-ounce container is dishwasher safe. A variable speed dial offers ten settings, and a separate switch engages pulse. The power cord measures 4.5 feet. Included accessories: the motor base, the 48-ounce container, and a mini-tamper for moving thick mixtures toward the blades. The unit carries a five-year full warranty covering parts, performance, labor, and shipping. Maintenance involves running the container with a drop of dish soap and warm water for 30 to 60 seconds, which cleans the blades and walls without disassembly, then rinsing the container and lid and leaving both to air dry before the next use.
