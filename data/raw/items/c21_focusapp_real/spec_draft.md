Forest is a focus timer in which a tree grows for the length of a session and dies if the user leaves the app, a mechanic reviewers report as motivating against a plain timer. Deep Focus blocks selected apps and websites during a session, with custom allowlists, and Time Guard schedules blocks outside sessions; reviewers describe this blocking as building better phone habits. Plant Together runs a shared group session in which the group's tree dies if anyone gives up. Mindful Space adds a breathing exercise and ambient soundscapes play alongside the timer. The free tier covers the timer, Deep Focus blocking, group sessions, and one soundscape. Focus analytics, custom tags, and the full sound library sit on the paid tier, and the browser extension is purchased separately from the mobile app. Pricing is split by platform, and the legacy one-time purchases have been discontinued in favour of a subscription. Coins earned in the app fund real tree planting through a partnership with Trees for the Future. Clients cover iOS, Android, and browser extensions, syncing across them. Sessions and forests sync to the account across devices, downloads reach 60 million, and leaving the app is what kills the growing tree. Allowlists are set up before a session begins.
