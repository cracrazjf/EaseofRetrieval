Forest is a focus timer with clients for iOS and Android and a browser extension; sessions and forests sync across those clients through a user account. A virtual tree grows for the length of a session and dies if the user leaves the app before the timer ends; leaving the app mid-session kills the growing tree. The growing tree motivates longer sessions than a plain countdown timer. Deep Focus blocks selected apps and websites while a session runs, working from custom allowlists that are configured before a session begins and cannot be changed once it is running; that blocking builds steadier phone habits over weeks. Time Guard applies focus schedules automatically outside sessions. Plant Together runs a shared group session in which the group tree dies if any member gives up before the timer ends. Mindful Space adds a guided breathing exercise, and ambient soundscapes play alongside the running timer. Coins earned in sessions unlock further tree species and fund real tree planting through a partnership with Trees for the Future, which has planted over two million trees. Downloads exceed sixty million. There is no standalone desktop application; desktop work runs through the browser extension. The free tier covers the timer, Deep Focus blocking, group sessions, and one soundscape. Focus analytics, custom tags, and the remaining soundscapes are not on the free tier and require the paid tier; the browser extension is not covered by a mobile purchase and is bought separately. Pricing is split by platform, an arrangement that is confusing for new users, and the legacy one-time purchase has been discontinued in favour of a subscription. Cross-device sync requires an account, which carries a record of completed sessions between the clients as a forest. Allowlisted apps open during a session without ending it. A session that reaches the end adds its tree to the forest for that day. There is no standalone desktop application, so desktop work runs through the browser extension, and session data cannot be exported out of the app.
