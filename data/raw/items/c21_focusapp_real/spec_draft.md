Forest is a focus-timer application built around a tree-planting mechanic. Starting a session plants a virtual tree that grows while the user stays in the app and withers if the user leaves before the timer ends. Deep Focus mode blocks other apps during sessions, with allowlists for apps the user permits, and Time Guard applies focus schedules automatically. Plant Together runs shared group sessions in which participants plant at the same time. Mindful Space and a set of ambient soundscapes accompany sessions. Through a partnership with Trees for the Future, in-app activity contributes to planting real trees; more than two million have been planted, and the app reports over 60 million downloads. The free tier includes the timer, Deep Focus blocking, group planting, and one soundscape; remaining features require the Plus subscription, and the earlier one-time Pro purchase is discontinued. Forest runs on iOS, Android, and a browser extension, with cross-device sync.
