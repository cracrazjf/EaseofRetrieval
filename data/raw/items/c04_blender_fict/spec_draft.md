The Quarvel BX5 is a countertop blender for home use. It measures 9-1/2 by 8-1/2 by 19 inches and weighs 11.2 pounds. A 1.8-horsepower motor runs on 110- to 120-volt household power and drives stainless steel blades through a metal drive coupling. The 64-ounce container is dishwasher safe and closes with a lid that has a removable fill cap. A speed dial offers eight settings, and three buttons run preset programs for smoothies, frozen desserts, and soups. The power cord measures four feet. Included accessories: the motor base, the 64-ounce container, and the lid with fill cap. The unit carries a three-year warranty covering parts and labor. Maintenance involves running the container with a drop of dish soap and warm water for about 60 seconds, then rinsing the container and lid and leaving both parts to air dry fully before the next use.
