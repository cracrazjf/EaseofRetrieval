The Quarvel BX5 is a countertop blender for home use. It measures 9-1/2 by 8-1/2 by 19 inches assembled and weighs 11.2 pounds, a mass that keeps the base planted while the motor runs. A 1.8-horsepower motor runs on 110- to 120-volt power and drives stainless steel blades through a metal drive coupling. The blade assembly is fixed in the container and does not come out for washing. A speed dial offers eight variable speeds, and three buttons run preset programs for smoothies, frozen desserts, and soups. There is no separate pulse switch on the panel. The 64-ounce container is dishwasher safe and closes with a lid carrying a removable fill cap, so ingredients go in while the blades turn. It is the only container size supplied, and a jar this size needs a certain volume before ingredients circulate. No tamper is supplied for pushing thick mixtures toward the blades. The power cord measures four feet, which fixes how far the base can sit from an outlet. Non-slip feet on the motor base hold it against the counter, and the footprint stays under ten inches across. At an assembled height of 19 inches the machine stands taller than a low shelf. Included accessories: the motor base, the 64-ounce container, and the lid with fill cap. The unit carries a three-year warranty covering parts and labour; the warranty does not cover shipping or performance. Maintenance involves running the container with dish soap and warm water for about 60 seconds, then rinsing the container and lid and leaving both parts to air dry fully before the next use. The container, the lid, and the fill cap are the only parts that come apart for washing, and none of them need tools. The dial, the three program buttons, and the on-off switch are the whole of the control panel, and the panel carries no timer or display of any kind.
