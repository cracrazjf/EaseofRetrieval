The Quarvel BX5 is a countertop blender for home use. It measures 9-1/2 by 8-1/2 by 19 inches assembled and weighs 11.2 pounds. A 1.8-horsepower motor runs on 110- to 120-volt power and drives stainless steel blades through a metal drive coupling. The 64-ounce container is dishwasher safe and closes with a lid carrying a removable fill cap; it is the only container size supplied, and a jar this size needs a certain volume before ingredients circulate. A speed dial offers eight settings and three buttons run preset programs for smoothies, frozen desserts, and soups, which covers a narrow set of recipes. No tamper is supplied for pushing down thick mixtures. The power cord measures four feet, which limits where the base can sit. Included accessories: the motor base, the 64-ounce container, and the lid with fill cap. The unit carries a three-year warranty covering parts and labour. Maintenance involves running the container with dish soap and warm water for about 60 seconds, then rinsing the container and lid and leaving both parts to air dry fully before the next use. The container, lid, and fill cap are the only parts that come apart for washing, and none of them need tools.
